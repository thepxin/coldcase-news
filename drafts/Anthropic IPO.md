# Anthropic IPO prices 980 million shares at $83 on Nasdaq

> **Then vs. Now (News report):**
> * **Then:** On November 10, 2026, Anthropic launched its IPO roadshow with a proposed price range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million Class A shares.
> * **Now:** On November 19, 2026, Anthropic began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol `ANT` after pricing 980 million Class A shares at $83.00 per share.

Anthropic, PBC began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol `ANT` on November 19, 2026, following pricing of 980 million Class A shares at $83.00 per share. Shares opened at $111.50 on November 19, closed their first trading day at $97.17, and closed at $88.91 on November 20.

## Timeline

* **2026-05-28**: Anthropic announced a $65 billion Series H financing round at a $965 billion post-money valuation.
* **2026-06-01**: Executives confidentially submitted a draft Form S-1 registration statement to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
* **2026-10-26**: Public filings on SEC EDGAR disclosed audited financial results for 2023 through 2025 and the `ANT` ticker symbol.
* **2026-10-31**: Annualized revenue run rate reached $81 billion.
* **2026-11-10**: Anthropic launched its IPO roadshow with an initial range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million shares.
* **2026-11-17**: Amended Form S-1/A-3 confirmed Nasdaq listing approval and aligned terminology with Executive Order 14434.
* **2026-11-18**: The SEC declared the registration statement effective, and underwriters priced 980 million shares at $83.00 per share.
* **2026-11-19**: Shares began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under `ANT`, opening at $111.50 and closing at $97.17.

## Details (as Reported)

* SEC filings for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, reported $33.24 billion in revenue, compared with $2.55 billion in the prior-year period.
* Those same nine-month filings reported a GAAP net loss of $104.04 billion, including $94.3 billion in non-cash fair-value adjustments tied to convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs.
* Operating losses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, totaled $1.26 billion versus $5.22 billion a year earlier, while cash and short-term investments stood at approximately $131.9 billion as of September 30, 2026.
* Reporting on the offering noted $91.6 billion in proceeds going to Anthropic and $1.9 billion to selling shareholders following the over-allotment exercise, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley serving as lead underwriters.
* Anthropic reached 11,900 customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue over the 12 months ended September 30, 2026, compared with more than 1,500 at year-end 2025.
* Google and Amazon rank as the largest outside shareholders, while co-founders hold 50.1% of voting power.
* Early Anthropic backer Dustin Moskovitz told *The New York Times* that frontier AI development is moving too fast and urged slower pacing.
