# Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after board vote

> **Then vs. Now (News report):**
> * **Then:** On November 17, 2026, Nvidia board chair Harvey C. Jones stated on the company's earnings call that directors had accepted Jensen Huang's resignation and did "not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO"<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>.
> * **Now:** Following a board vote on November 22 and input from senior levels of the U.S. government, Nvidia reinstated Huang as president, chief executive officer, and board director effective immediately on November 22, announced November 23<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a>, <a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>.

Nvidia announced on November 23, 2026, that it had reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer, and board member<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a>, <a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>. Shares in the chipmaker rose 31.48% to close at $165.05 on November 23<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a>, <a href="#src-5">5</a></sup>.

## Timeline

* **2026-11-11**: Nvidia's board accepted Huang's resignation from his roles as president, chief executive officer, and director, naming CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO and appointing Harvey C. Jones as chair of the board<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>.
* **2026-11-12**: Nvidia disclosed the executive transition, while shares closed at $166.19, down 4.34%<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a>, <a href="#src-5">5</a></sup>.
* **2026-11-17**: Under Kress's interim leadership, Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue, formed a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, and stated on its earnings call that it did not intend to reinstate Huang<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a>, <a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>.
* **2026-11-20**: Nvidia shares closed at $125.53, down 14.01%<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a>, <a href="#src-5">5</a></sup>.
* **2026-11-22**: Following meetings with Huang and input from senior levels of the U.S. government, the board voted unanimously to reinstate him effective immediately<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a>, <a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>.
* **2026-11-23**: Nvidia announced Huang's reinstatement, and shares closed at $165.05, up 31.48%<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a>, <a href="#src-4">4</a>, <a href="#src-5">5</a></sup>.

## Details (as Reported)

* In its November 23 announcement, Nvidia stated that directors held a series of discussions and meetings with Huang and were satisfied with his candor and ability to lead the company<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>.
* Chair Harvey C. Jones said, "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company," and added, "We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO"<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>.
* Huang stated in the company's announcement that he took the responsibility with humility and renewed commitment, thanking the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>.
* Coverage on November 23 noted that Nvidia's board sought national security assurances from senior levels of the U.S. government, while President Donald Trump spoke with Huang on November 22 ahead of the reinstatement announcement<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a>, <a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>.
* Alongside Huang's return, the board confirmed it is recruiting for a newly created Chief Compliance Officer position reporting directly to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>.
* Nvidia's announcement reiterated that its committee comprised solely of independent directors—with independent counsel assisting—will continue its investigation into reported foreign intelligence targeting, and that Huang will cooperate fully<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>.

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

* Nvidia's independent board committee investigation into reported foreign intelligence targeting remains open, and the company has not announced a completion date or public findings<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>.
* Neither Nvidia nor the White House has named the specific senior U.S. government officials who provided national security input to the board during its deliberations<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a>, <a href="#src-2">2</a>, <a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>.
