# Meta AI chief Alexandr Wang warns on superintelligence after Claude math claim

> **Then vs. Now (News report):**
> * **Then:** Frontier AI discussions centered on model benchmarks, enterprise tooling, and multi-year timelines toward advanced systems.
> * **Now:** On November 22 and November 23, 2026, Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang warned that "artificial superintelligence" is arriving and urged industry-wide shifts away from the default path after an Anthropic employee reported using Claude to help solve the Riemann hypothesis.

Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang warned on social media on November 22, 2026, that "artificial superintelligence" is arriving, following an Anthropic employee's announcement that he used Claude to solve the Riemann hypothesis. Wang stated that humankind's actions over the next year will carry major consequences and argued the industry must take meaningfully different steps from its current path.

## Timeline

* **2026-11-22**: An Anthropic employee announced he had used Claude to solve the Riemann hypothesis, and Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang posted his warning on the arrival of artificial superintelligence.
* **2026-11-23**: *Al Jazeera* and *The Guardian* reported on Wang's warning, alongside verification efforts by two groups regarding the Claude-assisted mathematics claim.

## Details (as Reported)

* *Al Jazeera* reported that Wang issued his warning on Sunday, November 22, 2026, shortly after the Anthropic employee's claim regarding the Riemann hypothesis.
* Wang stated that he strongly believes superintelligence is possible, while adding that achieving it likely involves the entire industry taking meaningfully different actions from the default path ahead.
* Reporting noted that the warning arrived amid broader debates over AI governance, chip export controls, and market volatility in AI-linked stocks.
* Separate coverage noted that two groups were reviewing the mathematician's claim that Claude helped prove the mathematical problem.

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

* Independent verification of the claimed Claude-assisted solution to the Riemann hypothesis has not been finalized.
* Specific industry-wide policy or engineering changes resulting from Wang's remarks have not been announced.
