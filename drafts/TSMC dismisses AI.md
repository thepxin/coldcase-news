# TSMC dismisses AI market correction fears as SPIL scales packaging

> **Then vs. Now (News report):**
> * **Then:** Tech equities faced sharp volatility and questions over whether AI infrastructure spending could sustain long-term returns.
> * **Now:** On November 24, 2026, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. dismissed concerns about a deeper AI market correction as demand outstripped advanced chip supply, while Siliconware Precision Industries Co. moved to triple its advanced packaging capacity by mid-2029.

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC) on November 24, 2026, dismissed concerns about a deeper correction in the artificial intelligence market, stating that global demand for AI applications continues to outstrip its supply of advanced chips. Separately, Siliconware Precision Industries Co. (SPIL) announced a multiyear expansion with Nvidia to triple its advanced packaging capacity by the middle of 2029.

## Timeline

* **2026-11-18**: SPIL unveiled a multibillion-dollar agreement with Nvidia to expand advanced packaging capacity in Taiwan.
* **2026-11-20**: Reporting indicated TSMC tightened restrictions on orders at 16 nanometers and below for affected customers without U.S. export licenses.
* **2026-11-23 to 2026-11-24**: TSMC dismissed deeper AI market correction concerns as demand exceeded advanced chip supply, while Asian equity markets and TSMC shares rebounded sharply.

## Details (as Reported)

* TSMC stated that soaring global demand for AI applications continues to outstrip its supply of advanced chips.
* Under the agreement with Nvidia, SPIL plans a multiyear expansion to triple its advanced packaging capacity by the middle of 2029, covering new production lines for bumping, assembly, and testing, facility upgrades, interposer wafer fabrication, and automation.
* An Nvidia executive said the partnership helps secure the advanced packaging capacity and turnkey production needed to meet demand.
* Reporting on November 20 indicated TSMC instructed that affected orders at 16 nanometers and below be halted unless customers hold U.S. export licenses.
* Market coverage on November 24 noted that Taiwan's stock market surged roughly 9% and TSMC shares climbed nearly 10% amid renewed AI infrastructure momentum.

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

* Specific quarterly revenue contributions from SPIL's tripling of advanced packaging capacity through mid-2029 have not yet been realized.
* Whether supply-demand balance in advanced AI chips will shift over 2027 remains subject to ongoing customer orders and regulatory rules.
