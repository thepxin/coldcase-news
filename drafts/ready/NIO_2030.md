# NIO sets 2030 goal of more than 10,000 battery swap stations after missing its last target
type: report
then: At its 2021 Power Day, NIO committed to more than 4,000 battery swap stations worldwide by the end of 2025 ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/nio-10000-battery-swap-stations-52-billion-batteries-2030/)).
now: On November 25, 2026, NIO announced that NIO Power expects to operate more than 10,000 battery swap stations and manage more than 350 billion renminbi in battery assets by 2030 ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/nio-10000-battery-swap-stations-52-billion-batteries-2030/)).
summary: NIO says its NIO Power unit expects to operate more than 10,000 battery swap stations and manage over 350 billion renminbi in battery assets by 2030.

NIO announced on November 25, 2026, at its NIO IN 2026 technology event in Guangzhou, that its energy arm, NIO Power, expects to operate more than 10,000 battery swap stations and to manage more than 350 billion renminbi ($52 billion) in battery assets by 2030 ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/nio-10000-battery-swap-stations-52-billion-batteries-2030/)). At the same event, the company unveiled NIO WorldModel 3.0, the latest version of its smart driving model, together with NOMI Intelligence 4.0 ([NIO](https://www.nio.com/news/20261125001)).

Electrek reported that the new station target is more than double the swap network the company currently runs in China, and that the company has already missed its last major swap station target ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/nio-10000-battery-swap-stations-52-billion-batteries-2030/)). At its 2021 Power Day, the company committed to more than 4,000 battery swap stations worldwide by the end of 2025; by February 2026, it had about 3,700, almost all of them in China ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/nio-10000-battery-swap-stations-52-billion-batteries-2030/)). As of November 11, the network stood at 4,252 battery swap stations in China ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/nio-10000-battery-swap-stations-52-billion-batteries-2030/)).

NIO WorldModel first appeared in July 2024 and has evolved through three iterations, the company stated ([NIO](https://www.nio.com/news/20261125001)). Beginning in December, version 3.0 is scheduled to reach all users of the Banyan, Cedar, Cedar S and Coconut+ smart systems, subject to regulatory filing and clearance ([NIO](https://www.nio.com/news/20261125001)). Cumulative deliveries across the company reached 1,332,139 vehicles as of October 31, 2026 ([NIO](https://www.nio.com/news/20261125001)).
