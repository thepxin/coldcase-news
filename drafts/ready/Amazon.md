# Amazon agrees to buy Decart optimization technology and team in reported $7.5 billion deal
type: report

Amazon announced on November 23, 2026, that it had signed an agreement to purchase the proprietary technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack and to bring Decart's co-founders, Dean Leitersdorf, Moshe Shalev and Orian Leitersdorf, to Amazon together with their team of artificial intelligence researchers and engineers [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-decart-ai-optimization). TechCrunch reported, citing Bloomberg, that Amazon is paying $7.5 billion [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/amazon-snaps-up-decarts-ai-optimization-tech-for-7-5b-as-it-prepares-to-sell-trainium-chips/).

## Details (as Reported)

- Amazon stated that the technology will help customers "train and run AI models faster and at lower cost" [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-decart-ai-optimization).
- Decart describes the Decart Optimization Stack as "hardware-agnostic from the ground up, tuning each model to every chip" [Decart](https://decart.ai/optimization-engine).
- TechCrunch reported that the software helps artificial intelligence models run efficiently on hardware from NVIDIA, Amazon and Google [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/amazon-snaps-up-decarts-ai-optimization-tech-for-7-5b-as-it-prepares-to-sell-trainium-chips/).
- Decart states that the stack can deliver "up to 3× faster responses per user than leading systems" [Decart](https://decart.ai/optimization-engine).
- According to Decart, its Lucy model edits live video in under 40 milliseconds at up to 100 frames per second, and its Oasis model responds to every action in under 200 milliseconds [Decart](https://decart.ai/optimization-engine).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed
