OpenAI has begun rolling out a combined Chat and Work experience in ChatGPT to a small group of Pro subscribers, TechCrunch reported on November 25, 2026 [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/).  A company spokesperson told the publication that most users are not expected to face more frequent usage resets, but that a small fraction of its heaviest users may hit their limits sooner [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/). 

The rollout follows an email in which the company informed users that it would begin testing the combined experience in the coming weeks, starting with a small group of users, as 9to5Mac reported on November 2, 2026 [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/02/openai-is-retiring-chatgpt-classic-app-will-soon-combine-chat-and-work-tabs-in-new-app/).  In that email, the company wrote that "most users won't need to wait for their usage to reset more often than they do today" [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/02/openai-is-retiring-chatgpt-classic-app-will-soon-combine-chat-and-work-tabs-in-new-app/). 

Some ChatGPT subscribers had raised concerns on social media and on the company's community forum that merging the usage limits for Chat and Work could leave them with less access to advanced reasoning models, even as they continue to pay the same subscription fees [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/).

## Details (as Reported)

- Product lead Thibault Sottiaux announced the start of the rollout to a small group of Pro subscribers in a post on X, according to TechCrunch [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/). 
- Under the new system, Chat and Work will share a single usage meter across ChatGPT, a company spokesperson told TechCrunch [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/). 
- Conversations using the Instant model will remain unlimited, will not count toward the shared allowance, and will stay available after users exhaust their usage limits [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/). 
- The spokesperson added that the company would continue working to improve efficiency so that subscribers could get more out of their plans [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/). 
- The company's help center states that, starting in December, the usage allowance on Plus, Pro and Business plans will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex as it brings "the agentic capabilities of Work into the main ChatGPT experience" [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625-understanding-chatgpt-and-codex-usage-limits). 
- The help center also states that usage will be measured in tokens and that more advanced models and complex tasks generally use more of the allowance [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625-understanding-chatgpt-and-codex-usage-limits). 
- A small group of users may see the usage changes before December so that the company can learn from early feedback, according to the help center [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625-understanding-chatgpt-and-codex-usage-limits). 
- The company plans to bring shared usage limits to users more broadly in December, TechCrunch reported, citing the support page [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/). 
- In its email to users, the company wrote that "Codex will remain a separate experience in the ChatGPT desktop app" [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/02/openai-is-retiring-chatgpt-classic-app-will-soon-combine-chat-and-work-tabs-in-new-app/).
