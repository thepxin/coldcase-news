# Nvidia board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after saying it did not intend to
type: closed

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer and board member on November 22, 2026 [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nvda-20261122.htm). On the company's November 17 earnings call, board chair Harvey C. Jones had stated that the board "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO" [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3189778). The company announced the unanimous vote on November 23, and the chipmaker's shares closed that day at $165.05, up 31.48% [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director), [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/history/).

## Timeline

- **2026-11-11**: The board accepted Huang's resignation as president, chief executive officer and director, appointed Colette Kress interim CEO and appointed Harvey C. Jones chair of the board ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm)).
- **2026-11-12**: The company filed a Form 8-K reporting the changes, and its shares closed at $166.19, down 4.34% ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm); [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/history/)).
- **2026-11-17**: The company reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion and established the National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, and Jones stated on the earnings call that the board did not intend to reinstate Huang ([NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/); [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-of-Directors-Establishes-National-Security-and-Export-Control-Oversight-Committee/default.aspx); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3189778)).
- **2026-11-20**: The shares closed at $125.53, down 14.01% ([Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/history/)).
- **2026-11-22**: The board reinstated Huang as president, chief executive officer and director ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nvda-20261122.htm)).
- **2026-11-23**: The company announced the unanimous vote, and the shares closed at $165.05, up 31.48% ([NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director); [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/history/)).

## Details (as Reported)

- The board stated that it voted following a series of discussions and meetings with Huang, after which it was satisfied with his candor and ability to lead the company [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
- The board reported receiving input from "senior levels of the United States government" and stated that, based on that input, it is confident the reinstatement poses no national security risk [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
- Jones stated: "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company." [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
- Jones also thanked the interim chief executive: "We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO" [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
- Huang stated that he takes on the responsibility "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment" and added: "I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process." [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
- The company confirmed it is searching for a new Chief Compliance Officer who will report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/).
- A committee composed solely of independent directors, assisted by independent counsel, is investigating reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, and Huang fully supports the investigation and will cooperate, according to the announcement [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- The board stated that the independent committee's investigation into the reported foreign intelligence effort will proceed to conclusion [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
- The company has not yet named a Chief Compliance Officer and stated that the search is under way [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/).
