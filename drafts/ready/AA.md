# NVIDIA reinstates Jensen Huang as chief executive officer after saying it would not
type: closed

NVIDIA announced on November 23, 2026, that its board had voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, effective immediately [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/). The announcement followed a statement on the company's earnings call on November 17, 2026, in which the board chair, Harvey Jones, said that the board "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO" [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2319982/nvidias-board-chair-says-the-company-is-not-considering-reinstating-jensen-huang-as-ceo). Shares in the chipmaker closed at $165.05 on November 23, 2026, up 31.48 per cent [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/history/).

## Details (as Reported)

- The board voted following a series of discussions and meetings with Huang, which left it satisfied with respect to his "candor and ability to lead NVIDIA", the company announced [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/).
- The company stated that the board received input from "senior levels of the United States government" and is confident that the reinstatement "poses no national security risk" [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/).
- Jones said, "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company", and added that the board is "grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO" [Securities and Exchange Commission filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm).
- Huang stated, "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment", and thanked the board for "its confidence and deliberation through this process" [Securities and Exchange Commission filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm).
- A committee comprised solely of independent directors, which has retained independent counsel, is investigating reports that Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, according to the company [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
- The company confirmed that Huang "fully supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it" [Securities and Exchange Commission filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm).
- The board has formed a National Security and Export Control Committee, comprised solely of independent directors, "to oversee trade compliance and national security risks", the company reported [Securities and Exchange Commission filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm).
- The company announced a search to fill a new position, Chief Compliance Officer, which will report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee [Securities and Exchange Commission filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- The investigation by independent directors into reports that Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort remains open, and the company stated that it "will proceed to conclusion" [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/).
- The company stated that it "will continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries" [Securities and Exchange Commission filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm).
