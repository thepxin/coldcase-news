# Amazon plans $7 billion renewable energy investment in India by 2030
type: report
then: Amazon stated that it had already contracted $4 billion in renewable energy investments in India [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
now: Amazon has announced plans to invest $7 billion in renewable energy projects in India by 2030 [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
summary: Amazon plans to invest $7 billion in renewable energy projects in India by 2030 and has set a global goal for the carbon intensity of its electricity.

Amazon has announced plans to invest $7 billion in renewable energy projects in India by 2030 [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy). The company stated that this sum is in addition to the $4 billion it has already contracted in renewable energy investments in the country, and it announced four new utility-scale wind and solar projects there [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy). Alongside its plans for India, the company set a new global goal to reduce the carbon intensity of the electricity powering its operations by 65 per cent by 2030, compared with a 2019 baseline [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).

## Details (as Reported)

- Amazon stated that its renewable energy portfolio in India includes 14 utility-scale wind and solar projects that add carbon-free energy to the grid, along with 43 on-site solar installations across its facilities in the country [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
- The four new projects include what the company described as its largest wind project contracted in Asia to date [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
- Amazon stated that, once operational, its projects in India will have a combined capacity of more than 2 gigawatts, enough to generate electricity equivalent to the annual needs of more than 3.5 million Indian households [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
- Under the new global goal, the company stated, every unit of electricity it consumes will be associated with 65 per cent fewer carbon emissions compared with a 2019 baseline [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
- Amazon stated that the new goal supports its Climate Pledge goal of reaching net-zero carbon by 2040 [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
- Across the world, the company reported more than 700 projects with a combined capacity of more than 40 gigawatts, which it stated is enough to power more than 12.1 million homes in the United States [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-investment-india-renewable-energy).
