# \# Anthropic raises $81.3 billion, with $1.05 trillion to $1.08 trillion in infrastructure commitments

# type: closed

# 

# Anthropic became a public company on Thursday, November 19, 2026, raising a total of $81.3 billion, including $1.95 billion for its seven founders (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)).

# 

# TechCrunch reported that the offering was priced at $83 a share, above the initial range of $70 to $78, and that the company sold 980 million shares, up from the 825 million initially planned (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/?type=AI)). The sale surpassed the $75 billion record set by SpaceX, becoming the largest initial public offering in history by proceeds raised (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/?type=AI)).

# 

# On November 10, TechCrunch reported that each of the seven co-founders would sell 3,378,378 shares, which at the $74 midpoint of the range would bring each of them just shy of $250 million (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/all-7-anthropic-founders-will-each-cash-out-about-250m-in-ipo/)). At the final price, each founder took in about $280 million before underwriting fees and taxes (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)).

# 

# According to TechCrunch, the company raised nearly $105 billion for its own coffers, a figure that could rise to $117 billion if the bankers buy all of their allotment of shares (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)). The raise gives the company nearly $230 billion of working capital (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)). Against that sum, the company carries infrastructure commitments in the region of $1.05 trillion to $1.08 trillion (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)).

# 

# Amazon holds a 14.6 per cent stake and Google holds 13.4 per cent, so that the two cloud providers together own 28 per cent of the company (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)).

# 

# Speaking ahead of the Nasdaq opening bell, chief executive officer Dario Amodei pledged that "we will pace the training and deployment of AI systems" (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/at-nasdaq-opening-bell-anthropic-ceo-recommits-to-pacing-ai-development/)). He added that the company would not bend to business or political pressure (\[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/at-nasdaq-opening-bell-anthropic-ceo-recommits-to-pacing-ai-development/)).
