HEADLINE: Broken Embargo Unleashes 'Dirty Frag' Zero-Day Before Linux Patches Exist

A broken embargo leaked the devastating 'Dirty Frag' local privilege escalation flaw before kernel developers could finalize a fix. The bug grants trivial, immediate root access on virtually every Linux distribution released since 2017. Sysadmins are left scrambling to mitigate live exploits in the wild without official upstream patches.
