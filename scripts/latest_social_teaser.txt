HEADLINE: The AI Startup Graveyard: Why First-Wave AI Ventures Are Quietly Folding

Recent postmortems from startups like Cydoc and Plumb show that building AI models was only 20% of the operational challenge. Most ventures folded under enterprise integration debt, sales friction, and high customer acquisition costs. At the same time, open-source git standards and explicit 'anti-AI' software are taking their place.
