HEADLINE: Anatomy of Cloud Failures: How Google Deconstructed Its Multi-Hour Network and Compute Outages

Public post-mortems from Google Cloud highlight how automated resilience features can backfire into prolonged service outages. In one case, a live-migration bug silently detached load balancers from VMs for over 30 hours, while another incident briefly dropped transit globally across all regions. These analyses demonstrate why multi-layered monitoring and post-incident transparency remain essential for enterprise cloud operations.
