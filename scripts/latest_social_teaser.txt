HEADLINE: Cloud Outage Post-Mortems: How Transient Glitches Expose Deep Architectural Dependencies

Public cloud providers often market seamless isolation, but engineering post-mortems reveal that shared internal dependencies remain brittle points of failure. From Google Cloud identity hiccups to TeamCity RabbitMQ crashes, centralized control planes regularly drag independent workloads offline. Real-world resilience still depends on how gracefully services handle catastrophic backplane failures.
