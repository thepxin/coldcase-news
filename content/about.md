**ColdCaseTech** is an independent cybersecurity and cloud infrastructure intelligence desk. While mainstream tech media abandons major security incidents after 48 hours, we track the long-tail aftermath—forensic post-mortems, CVE remediation failures, SEC breach filings, and regulatory fallout—months later.

### Our Investigative Methodology
1. **Primary Forensic Sources:** Every analysis cross-references initial corporate disclosures against subsequent engineering post-mortems, CISA advisories, and court filings.
2. **Then vs. Now Auditing:** We grade early incident claims (`Verified`, `Partially True`, or `Escalated`) based on documented blast radius and root-cause transparency.
3. **Actionable Defense:** Each report pairs architectural lessons with vetted security hardware, zero-trust networking, and incident-response tooling.
