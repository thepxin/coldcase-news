<p class="rec-guide-intro">Launch pages are written to sound like promises. Most are carefully worded so they are not. Keep this page next to the next keynote you watch. <button class="rec-print" type="button" onclick="window.print()">Print or save as PDF</button></p>

<ol class="rec-guide">
<li><h2>"Up to"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> the best result the company measured, under conditions it chose. Not the typical result.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> the footnote that says how it was tested, and what independent reviewers measured.</p></li>

<li><h2>"As early as"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> the earliest possible date. It is not a promise of that date.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> whether a firm date, month or quarter is given anywhere else.</p></li>

<li><h2>"Later this year" · "Coming soon"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> "coming soon" commits to nothing. "Later this year" does set a deadline: December 31.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> whether it arrives by that deadline. This is exactly when we open a case.</p></li>

<li><h2>"Rolling out"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> a staged release. Some people get it today; you may wait weeks.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> which countries, devices or accounts come first, and when the rollout is complete.</p></li>

<li><h2>"In select markets" · "Where available"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> some countries, not all. Yours may not be on the list.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> the country list, usually on a support page rather than the launch page.</p></li>

<li><h2>"Starting at"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> the price of the cheapest version.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> the price of the version the reviews and benchmarks actually used.</p></li>

<li><h2>"Compared with the previous generation"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> a comparison against a baseline the company picked. Sometimes that is a model two or three years old.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> which model it was compared with, and on which test.</p></li>

<li><h2>"2x faster"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> a speed-up on one task or benchmark, not on everything.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> which task was measured, and whether it is a task you do.</p></li>

<li><h2>"Available to order"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> you can pay today. It does not mean it ships today.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> the shipping estimate on the order page.</p></li>

<li><h2>"Beta" · "Preview" · "Early access"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> an unfinished version that may change or be withdrawn.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> whether a date is given for the finished release.</p></li>

<li><h2>"Industry-leading" · "Best-in-class"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> nothing you can check, unless the company says what it was compared with.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> the named competitor and the named test. No names, no claim.</p></li>

<li><h2>"On track"</h2>
<p><b>What it commits to:</b> confidence today. It is not delivery.</p>
<p><b>What to check:</b> the next dated milestone, and whether that one is met.</p></li>
</ol>
