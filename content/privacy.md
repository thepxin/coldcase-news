### 1. FTC Affiliate & Material Connection Disclosure
In compliance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines (16 CFR Part 255), please note that **ColdCaseTech** is reader-supported. Many articles, post-mortem reports, and resource toolkits on this website contain outbound links (including branded `/go/` redirect links) to third-party cybersecurity software, cloud hosting providers, and hardware vendors.

If you click on one of these links and make a qualifying purchase or subscription within the merchant's referral window, ColdCaseTech may receive an affiliate commission at **no additional cost to you**. Our editorial verdicts, vulnerability assessments, and post-mortem grading (`Verified`, `Partially True`, `Escalated`) are written independently and are never influenced by affiliate partnerships.

### 3. Third-Party Affiliate Network Cookies
When you click an outbound partner recommendation or `/go/` link on ColdCaseTech, you may be routed through authorized affiliate attribution networks, including:
* **CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction):** Uses authorized tracking domains (such as `dpbolvw.net`, `anrdoezrs.net`, `jdoqocy.com`, and `kqzyfj.com`) to place a temporary referral attribution cookie (typically valid for 30 days) on your browser to credit ColdCaseTech for referred purchases (such as Surfshark or GearUP).
* **Awin, PartnerStack & Refersion:** Used to attribute referrals to cloud infrastructure, AI audio, and security hardware partners (such as DigitalOcean, ElevenLabs, and Hak5).
* **Amazon Associates:** Places a standard 24-hour session cookie when you click an Amazon product link.

These third-party network cookies do **not** give ColdCaseTech access to your name, email address, credit card details, or personal browsing history. You can clear or block these cookies at any time using your browser settings or privacy extensions.

### 4. Website Telemetry, APIs & Newsletter Data
* **Static Architecture:** ColdCaseTech is compiled as a static site via Hugo and hosted via GitHub Pages / Cloudflare. We do not run invasive behavioral ad trackers or sell user data to data brokers.
* **Live CVE Radar API:** When you visit our `/radar/` page, your browser makes a direct client-side request to public vulnerability feeds (such as CIRCL / NVD CVE APIs) to display real-time advisories.
* **Email Subscriptions:** If you voluntarily subscribe to our post-mortem intelligence brief, your email address is stored strictly to deliver our newsletter updates and can be unsubscribed with one click at any time.

### 5. GDPR, CCPA & Global Privacy Rights
Regardless of your geographic location (including the European Economic Area under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, or India under the DPDP Act), you have the right to browse ColdCaseTech with strict cookie-blocking shields enabled, opt out of any communications, or request the deletion of any email address voluntarily submitted to us.
