Google announced on 2026-11-17 that Google Wallet is now available on iOS for users across the U.S. ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/wallet-available-ios-united-states/)). The company said the app syncs with Gmail and other Google apps to keep package tracking, digital boarding passes, concert tickets and receipts available across devices ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/wallet-available-ios-united-states/)). Google also noted that tap-to-pay functionality isn't supported on iOS at this time ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/wallet-available-ios-united-states/)).

## Details (as Reported)

They can scan physical loyalty and gym cards with the phone's camera to import them, manage payment cards to check out faster online across Google and anywhere Google Pay is used, and view order confirmations, receipts and delivery updates for agentic purchases made across Google ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/wallet-available-ios-united-states/)). The company described Google Wallet as a future secure hub for managing agentic purchases across Google ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/wallet-available-ios-united-states/)).

A separate Gemini integration uses the Plaid service to link credit card and bank accounts to Google Wallet, according to 9to5Google ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/13/gemini-google-wallet/)). The integration is read-only, and Gemini cannot move, manage or invest money ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/13/gemini-google-wallet/)). Users choose whether to opt in and can turn the connection on or off at any time ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/13/gemini-google-wallet/)). Once enabled, it is available in the Gemini app on Android, iOS and web for U.S. users ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/13/gemini-google-wallet/)).

On the Apple side, Bloomberg reported that Fugman inherits a tap-to-pay business that handles tens of billions of transactions annually across more than 90 markets, with Apple taking a percentage ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/apples-leadership-overhaul-continues-with-new-apple-pay-and-wallet-chief/)). Bloomberg also reported that the division includes the Apple Card, which is transitioning from Goldman Sachs Group Inc. to JPMorgan Chase & Co. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/apples-leadership-overhaul-continues-with-new-apple-pay-and-wallet-chief/)).
