Apple unveiled the all-new MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display on October 28, 2026, and announced that availability begins Friday, November 6 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)). Pre-orders opened the same day in 14- and 16-inch models ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)). The laptop uses a tandem OLED display and brings Touch to the Mac for the first time ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)). 9to5Mac reported that the 14-inch model starts at $2899 and the 16-inch model at $3399 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/apple-announces-redesigned-macbook-pro-its-first-touchscreen-mac/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Apple described the display as offering up to 2000 nits peak HDR and SDR brightness, a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio and a 120Hz variable refresh rate ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)). It also covers both P3 and Adobe RGB color gamuts ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)). Apple added that the Dynamic Island comes to Mac as the home for Spotlight, Live Activities and Siri AI ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)).

The company listed M5 Pro and M5 Max chips, up to 128GB of unified memory and up to 16TB of storage ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)). Apple also cited up to 24 hours of battery life, Thunderbolt 5, a 12MP Center Stage camera and an enhanced six-speaker sound system ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)).

9to5Mac reported that the 14-inch model weighs 3 pounds, compared with 3.5 pounds for the same version of the previous design ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/apple-announces-redesigned-macbook-pro-its-first-touchscreen-mac/)). The 16-inch model weighs 4 pounds, compared with 4.7 pounds for the previous design ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/apple-announces-redesigned-macbook-pro-its-first-touchscreen-mac/)). Colors are silver or space black, with standard or nano-texture display configurations ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/apple-announces-redesigned-macbook-pro-its-first-touchscreen-mac/)).

The newsroom also reported that peak HDR display brightness rises from 1600 nits to 2000 nits ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/apple-announces-redesigned-macbook-pro-its-first-touchscreen-mac/)). The existing MacBook Pro configurations from March remain on sale at the same prices, without OLED, the glowing Apple logo or the thinner and lighter design ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/apple-announces-redesigned-macbook-pro-its-first-touchscreen-mac/)). The new machines are also available to order from Amazon ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/apple-announces-redesigned-macbook-pro-its-first-touchscreen-mac/)).
