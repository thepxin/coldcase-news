The FBI said in a May 15, 2026 public service announcement that a cyber-attack on an online Learning Management System interrupted service to educational institutions and students across the country, and that the platform was fully operational ([FBI](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260515)). The FBI said ShinyHunters claimed the attack and described the group as specializing in large-scale data breaches and extortion ([FBI](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260515)). By October 2026, Dark Reading reported that the FBI had been aggressively pursuing ShinyHunters and had made a number of arrests ([Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/insider-threats/prominent-ransomware-negotiator-extortion-charges)). KrebsOnSecurity reported that the group had hacked the FBI recruitment website fbijobs.gov in late September ([Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/)).

## Timeline

- **2026-05-03**: KrebsOnSecurity reported that the Shinywire victim extortion website became active around this date ([Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/)).
- **2026-05-15**: The FBI issued a PSA about the LMS cyber-attack claimed by ShinyHunters ([FBI](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260515)).
- **2026-09-15**: KrebsOnSecurity reported that Dutch authorities arrested ShinyHunters suspect Pepijn van der Stap ([Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/)).
- **2026-10-03**: KrebsOnSecurity reported that Reuters said Jordanian authorities had detained "Rey" ([Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/)).
- **2026-10-09**: Dark Reading reported that FBI Director Kash Patel posted on X that the bureau arrested another suspected ShinyHunters co-conspirator ([Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/insider-threats/prominent-ransomware-negotiator-extortion-charges)).
- **2026-10-09**: KrebsOnSecurity reported that the FBI arrested Edward Dubrovsky, 54, in connection with the ShinyHunters investigation ([Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/)).
- **2026-10-16**: KrebsOnSecurity reported that the Shinywire website was active until this date and is now defunct ([Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/)).

## Root Cause (as Reported)

Dark Reading reported that Patel described the FBIjobs.gov incident as occurring on a platform managed by a third-party vendor ([Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/insider-threats/prominent-ransomware-negotiator-extortion-charges)). KrebsOnSecurity reported that Intuitive Surgical, another ShinyHunters victim, was breached in March 2026 after an employee fell for a targeted phishing email ([Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/)). The FBI PSA does not state how the LMS platform was compromised ([FBI](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260515)).
