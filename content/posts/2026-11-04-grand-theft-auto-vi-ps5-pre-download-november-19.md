PlayStation announced on November 3 that Grand Theft Auto VI launches November 19 on PlayStation 5, with pre-download available starting November 10 ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)). The company published a four-step preparation guide covering account setup, pre-orders, storage and accessories ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)).

## Details (as Reported)

PlayStation advised players to confirm that their account details are up to date, and to create an account if they are new to the platform ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)). Pre-orders can be placed through PlayStation Store or participating retailers ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)).

The Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition includes an exclusive collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and action threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story, according to the company ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)). Pre-download timing is available to those who pre-order, starting at November 10 local midnight ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)). The company asks players to clear a minimum of 212GB on the PS5 and points to options for extending PS5 storage ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)).

PlayStation said downloading before release means players can start as soon as the game becomes available on November 19 ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)). Its guide also lists the DualSense Edge wireless controller, the PlayStation Pulse wireless headset and Pulse Elevate Wireless speakers as accessories for setup ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/)).

The Verge noted that the Ocarina of Time remake, scheduled for November 5, is one of just a few big-budget titles launching in the same month as Grand Theft Auto VI ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1026112/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-nintendo-switch-2-news)).
