Apple announced on November 10, 2026 that four new games will arrive on Apple Arcade on December 3 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). Zombies: Replanted, solitaire(ish), Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown+ and LEGO DUPLO Playtime ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). The company also stated that Football Manager 27 Touch is available on the service starting November 10, 2026 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Apple described Football Manager 27 Touch as having an elevated Match Engine, new Recruitment and Match Preparation tools, and a redesigned iPhone interface ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). The game joins NBA 2K27 Arcade Edition, Madden NFL 27 Arcade Edition, NFL Retro Bowl '27, PGA TOUR Pro Golf and Retro Bowl College+ among the sports games on Arcade ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)).

Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted adds new levels, fresh modes, and local co-op and PvP on the same device, along with an Arcade-exclusive Party mode ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). Playstack, the publisher of Balatro, brings solitaire(ish), in which players build card stacks to hit rising score goals and upgrade their decks from more than 150 special cards ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown+ arrives with all downloadable content included, alongside new challenges, quests and cosmetic updates ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). LEGO DUPLO Playtime is a digital toy chest where players mix and match LEGO DUPLO bricks ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)).

One Apple Arcade subscription covers a family of up to six ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). Customers who buy a new iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV receive three months of Apple Arcade for free ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)). Apple One plans that include Arcade are priced at $21.95 (Individual), $27.95 (Family) and $39.95 (Premier) in the U.S. ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/football-manager-and-other-hit-franchises-headline-apple-arcades-holiday-lineup/)).
