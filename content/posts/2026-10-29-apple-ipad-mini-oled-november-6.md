Apple announced an iPad mini with an 8.5-inch OLED display this week, priced from $699, with preorders open ahead of wide availability on November 6 ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)). The tablet was the first of several Apple announcements in the week of the company's "Pro and Tell" event in Los Angeles ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Engadget described the iPad mini as upgraded to an 8.5-inch OLED display, available with a nano-texture coating ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)). Inside is an A20 chip, the same system on a chip that debuted earlier this year alongside the iPhone 18 Pro ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)). Pricing starts at $699, with preorders opening ahead of wide availability on November 6 ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)).

Apple's other announcements that week included an iMac with an M6 processor, which Engadget described as a big improvement over the M4-powered 2024 models ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)). Starting at $1,499, the iMac was set to open for preorder later that day ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)). The M6 first arrived on the Mac mini and Mac Studio weeks earlier ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)).

The M6 upgrade for the MacBook Pro applies only to the 14-inch model, which starts at $1,999 with 16GB of unified memory and a 1TB SSD, while the 16-inch version is powered by either an M5 Pro or an M5 Max chip ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)).

- Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported that Apple has discussed charging $2,899 for a 14-inch touchscreen MacBook Pro with an M5 Pro chip, according to his sources ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/27/last-minute-leak-reveals-new-touchscreen-macbook-pro-details-pricing-new-cooling-system-more/)).
- The same report states that preorders for the touchscreen models would open on October 28, with availability potentially beginning as early as November 6 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/27/last-minute-leak-reveals-new-touchscreen-macbook-pro-details-pricing-new-cooling-system-more/)).
