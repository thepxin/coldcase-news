Apple announced on November 3, 2026 that its redesigned Health app will expand language support to global English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), French, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Turkish, and Vietnamese ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). The company stated that the app is available in the iOS 27.3 developer beta today and will roll out to users as a free software update later this year ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). On November 5, 9to5Mac reported that Apple had released the first iOS 27.3 public beta for iPhone, which brings back the redesigned Health app that briefly appeared during iOS 27.2 testing ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/05/apple-releases-first-ios-27-3-public-beta-with-redesigned-health-app-and-more/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Apple described the redesign as built with Apple Intelligence at its core, adapting to an individual's health metrics across iPhone and iPad ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). A new Insights tab surfaces summaries spanning heart, sleep, readiness, fitness, vitals, and cycle tracking data, according to the company ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). A For You section delivers recommendations based on a user's daily habits ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)).

A new Longevity tab analyzes longitudinal metrics across heart health, sleep, mental wellbeing, movement, metabolic health, hearing, and nutrition ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). Its central feature, Health Age, translates Apple Watch data such as VO2 max, resting heart rate, sleep, and heart rate variability into a metric relative to chronological age ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). Users can also add clinical laboratory data, such as A1c and LDL cholesterol ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)).

The update also introduces at-home movement evaluations that use an iPhone camera and Apple Watch to measure flexibility, strength, balance, movement mechanics, and VO2 max ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). Apple stated that the experience runs entirely on device and that no video is recorded, stored, or shared ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)). The VO2 max test requires Apple Watch, AirPods Pro 3, or a third-party heart rate sensing device, and Health Age requires Apple Watch ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/redesigned-health-app-expands-language-support/)).

9to5Mac reported that the redesigned Health app did not make it into the final iOS 27.2 release ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/05/apple-releases-first-ios-27-3-public-beta-with-redesigned-health-app-and-more/)). The outlet also listed other iOS 27.3 beta changes, including vertical tabs in Safari, new Siri AI actions in Apple apps, and Reachability on the iPhone Duo ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/05/apple-releases-first-ios-27-3-public-beta-with-redesigned-health-app-and-more/)).
