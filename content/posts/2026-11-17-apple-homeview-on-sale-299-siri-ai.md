Apple began selling the HomeView smart display on November 13, 2026, at Apple Store locations, on the Apple Store online and in the Apple Store app in select regions ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)). The company describes the device as powered by Apple Intelligence with Siri AI and priced it at $299 in the U.S. ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)). It is not on sale in the European Union ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Apple stated that HomeView brings entertainment, connection and smart home management together, from music and FaceTime calls to home monitoring and hands-free voice automations ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)). It recognizes up to six people by face and shows their personal content in Messages, FaceTime, Calendar, Reminders and Find My ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)). Siri AI has onscreen awareness, and when paired with an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone or iPad it can draw on personal context such as Calendar and Messages ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)).

Apple stated that personal content appears only when a user is recognized by face and their paired iPhone or iPad is present and recently unlocked ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)). The tabletop model comes in silver, starlight, rose pink and space gray, and the wall-mounted model in silver and space gray ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)). Siri AI on HomeView is available in English in the U.S., Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa and the UK ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)).

In Canada, Apple priced HomeView and the wall-mounted model at $429 (CAD) ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/ca/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)). Apple's footnotes state that Siri AI is available in beta in HomeView software version 27.2, and that certain features relying on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)).

9to5Mac reported that HomeView has the same bug that tvOS 27 introduced to the Apple TV 4K, so shows added via URL do not work in the Podcasts app, while HLS-based video podcasts are supported ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/homeview-missing-some-podcast-feeds-heres-why/)). Wired reported that the HomeView person recognition technology is protected by the Secure Exclave in Apple's A20 Pro processor ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/iphone-duo-is-getting-a-limited-version-of-face-id/)).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- Apple stated that Canadian French-language support will be offered later via a free software update ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)).
- Apple stated that it is working constructively with the EU to find a path forward for Siri AI ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-homeview-available-today/)).
