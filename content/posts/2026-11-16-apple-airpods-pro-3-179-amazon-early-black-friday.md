Amazon cut Apple's AirPods Pro 3 to $179.99 shipped on November 15, 2026, from a regular price of $249, as part of its early Black Friday sale, according to 9to5Mac ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/airpods-pro-3-hit-black-friday-pricing-early-at-179-reg-249/)). The outlet described the price as among the best deals ever offered on the earbuds ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/airpods-pro-3-hit-black-friday-pricing-early-at-179-reg-249/)).

The new price follows a $199 listing that 9to5Mac tracked earlier in the week, when the AirPods Pro 3 sat $50 off ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/deals-apple-tv-4k-discount-airpods-pro-3-airtag-2/)).

## Timeline

- **2026-11-12**: 9to5Mac tracked AirPods Pro 3 at $50 off in Amazon's early Black Friday sale, listing them at $199 against a $249 regular price ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/deals-homeview-m5-ipad-pro-magic-keyboard-airtag-2/)).
- **2026-11-13**: Wired listed the AirPods Pro 3 at $200, which it put at $49 off, and reported the 20 percent discount at Amazon, Target and Walmart ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/best-early-black-friday-airpods-deals-11-13-2026/)).
- **2026-11-15**: 9to5Mac reported that the AirPods Pro 3 dropped to $179 shipped at Amazon ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/airpods-pro-3-hit-black-friday-pricing-early-at-179-reg-249/)).

## Details (as Reported)

9to5Mac reported that the $70 discount undercuts the deal it had been tracking on and off over the previous few weeks by $20 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/airpods-pro-3-hit-black-friday-pricing-early-at-179-reg-249/)). The outlet added that the price is on par with the fall Prime Day discount and the official summer Amazon sale ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/airpods-pro-3-hit-black-friday-pricing-early-at-179-reg-249/)). It also placed the price within $10 of the limited all-time low that jumped in and out of stock in late June ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/airpods-pro-3-hit-black-friday-pricing-early-at-179-reg-249/)).

Wired described the AirPods Pro 3 as having stronger noise canceling, a new acoustic architecture for deeper bass and redesigned ear tips ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/best-early-black-friday-airpods-deals-11-13-2026/)). It listed a built-in heart rate sensor, live translation and a camera remote, and called them the first AirPods with an IP57 rating against dust, sweat and rain ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/best-early-black-friday-airpods-deals-11-13-2026/)).

9to5Mac noted that the discount follows an iOS 27 update that brought Custom EQ support, GymKit integration and better Find My support to the earbuds ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/airpods-pro-3-hit-black-friday-pricing-early-at-179-reg-249/)). In the same sale, the AirPods Max 2 were listed at $449 against a regular $549 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/14/early-black-friday-apple-deals-apple-tv-4k-airpods/)).
