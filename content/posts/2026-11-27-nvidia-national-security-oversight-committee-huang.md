NVIDIA announced on November 17, 2026, that its board of directors had established a standing National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, chaired by director Suzanne Nora Johnson ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-of-directors-establishes-national-security-and-export-control-oversight-committee)). On November 23, 2026, the company announced that the board had voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, effective immediately ([NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/)).

According to the November 17 announcement, the committee is responsible for board-level oversight of the company's national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-of-directors-establishes-national-security-and-export-control-oversight-committee)). It will also oversee the company's ongoing efforts to enhance its internal National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team, which will report directly to the committee and to the interim chief executive officer, Colette Kress, on day-to-day operations ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-of-directors-establishes-national-security-and-export-control-oversight-committee)). Directors Stephen Neal and John Dabiri will serve as members alongside the chair ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-of-directors-establishes-national-security-and-export-control-oversight-committee)).

## Details (as Reported)

- The company stated that the committee will retain independent, outside experts in law, national security and trade compliance ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-of-directors-establishes-national-security-and-export-control-oversight-committee)).
- The November 17 announcement referred to the aim "to enhance its engagement with relevant government stakeholders" ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-of-directors-establishes-national-security-and-export-control-oversight-committee)).
- In its November 23 announcement, the company stated that the board had formed a National Security and Export Control Committee, made up solely of independent directors, "to oversee trade compliance and national security risks" ([Securities and Exchange Commission filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)).
