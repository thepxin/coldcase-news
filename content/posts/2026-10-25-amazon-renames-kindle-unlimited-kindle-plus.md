Amazon renamed its Kindle Unlimited subscription Kindle+ on October 22, 2026, according to an update on its own explainer page ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)). The service costs $11.99 per month plus taxes in the U.S. ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)). 9to5Mac reported that the unveiling is largely a rebrand of the existing service, with the same features and price but a larger audiobook library ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/amazon-unveils-kindle-subscription-a-rebrand-with-more-audiobooks/)).

## Details (as Reported)

- Amazon describes Kindle+ as a monthly subscription with access to millions of eBooks and thousands of audiobooks, comics, manga and magazines ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
- Subscribers can borrow up to 20 digital books, audiobooks or comics at a time and return them without due date restrictions while the subscription is active ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
- Thousands of eBooks include companion audiobooks at no additional cost ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
- The magazine selection includes People, Reader's Digest and Men's Health ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
- Kindle+ works on any Kindle device and through the free Kindle app on iOS, Android, Mac, PC and the web ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
- Kindle+ is not included with Prime, according to Amazon, though Prime members get a free book every month through Amazon First Reads and can explore a curated selection of the catalog ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
- Subscribers can cancel at any time and keep access until the next billing date, and Amazon does not refund subscription fees already paid ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
- Quoting ZDNet, 9to5Mac reported that authors now have the option to include more eligible audiobooks in Kindle+, including AI-narrated titles ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/amazon-unveils-kindle-subscription-a-rebrand-with-more-audiobooks/)).
- Virtual Voice is Amazon's tool for authors to offer AI-narrated versions of their books, and 9to5Mac reported that Kindle+ also adds more human-narrated audiobooks ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/amazon-unveils-kindle-subscription-a-rebrand-with-more-audiobooks/)).
- Amazon lists Kindle+ as available in Australia, Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, India, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Spain, the U.S., the UK, and many more countries and territories ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus)).
