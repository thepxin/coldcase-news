Delta, a retro game emulator, launched its 2.0 version on 2026-10-22, according to 9to5Mac ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). 9to5Mac reported that the update most notably features a refreshed Liquid Glass interface and support for the iPhone Duo ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). The report also lists Sega Genesis ROM compatibility, improved library navigation and updated audio settings ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

## Details (as Reported)

9to5Mac reported that the app now has specially designed controller skins optimized for all the different iPhone Duo poses ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). These include Nintendo DS layouts that use the full size of the inner display ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). The layouts span the crease when the device is partially folded in book mode in portrait ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

9to5Mac said the developers also ensured that the app reflows on the portrait closed outer screen ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). According to 9to5Mac, folding the Duo closed automatically puts the DS to sleep, emulating the physical behavior of a real DS console ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

9to5Mac reported a trick in landscape split view alongside another app ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). Because the app exposes the bottom half of the virtual DS as a custom keyboard view, the system allows this keyboard to stretch across the full width of the screen, according to 9to5Mac ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

On pricing, 9to5Mac said the premium version can be unlocked with an In-App Purchase subscription through the App Store, now available worldwide ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). In the US, EU, Brazil and Japan, users can also support the app directly through Patreon, according to 9to5Mac ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

Apple's App Store listing for Delta states that it supports save states, cheats, RetroAchievements and custom controller skins ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688)). The listing says Delta Sync supports both Google Drive and Dropbox ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688)). The listing also states that Delta is in no way affiliated with Nintendo ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688)).
