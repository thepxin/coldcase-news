Delta, the retro game emulator, launched version 2.0 on 2026-10-22, 9to5Mac reports ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). The headline changes are a refreshed Liquid Glass interface and support for the iPhone Duo ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). The report also lists Sega Genesis ROM compatibility, improved library navigation and updated audio settings ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The app now has controller skins designed and optimized for every iPhone Duo pose ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). They include Nintendo DS layouts that use the full size of the inner display ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). With the device partly folded in book mode in portrait, the layouts span the crease ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

The developers also made sure the app reflows on the outer screen when the Duo is closed in portrait ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). Folding the Duo shut puts the DS to sleep automatically, just as closing a real DS console does ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

9to5Mac points to a trick in landscape split view alongside another app ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). Because the app exposes the bottom half of the virtual DS as a custom keyboard view, the system lets that keyboard stretch across the full width of the screen ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

The premium version is unlocked with an In-App Purchase subscription through the App Store, now available worldwide ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)). In the US, EU, Brazil and Japan, users can also support the app directly through Patreon ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/retro-game-emulator-delta-looks-great-on-the-iphone-duo/)).

Apple's App Store listing says Delta supports save states, cheats, RetroAchievements and custom controller skins ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688)). Delta Sync works with both Google Drive and Dropbox ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688)). The listing also states that Delta is in no way affiliated with Nintendo ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688)).
