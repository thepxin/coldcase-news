Microsoft marked 25 years of Xbox on November 12, 2026 with a message to Team XBOX employees worldwide ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/25-years-of-xbox/)). The original console reached North America on Nov 15, 2001 ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/the-original-xbox-was-released-25-years-ago-today-microsofts-pentium-iii-and-nvidia-gpu-powered-console-did-surprisingly-well-in-a-ps2-dominated-era)). Microsoft is also offering a 25-game bundle with new Windows PCs and a limited-edition Xbox Series X25 ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/the-original-xbox-was-released-25-years-ago-today-microsofts-pentium-iii-and-nvidia-gpu-powered-console-did-surprisingly-well-in-a-ps2-dominated-era)).

## Details (as Reported)

In the employee message, Microsoft said Xbox has built four generations of consoles, with a fifth on the way ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/25-years-of-xbox/)). The message states that a small team will be able to build worlds that today demand hundreds of developers and hundreds of millions of dollars ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/25-years-of-xbox/)). It also notes that the 2024 Microsoft Flight Simulator draws on more than 2.5 petabytes of data, against 160 KB for the first game ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/25-years-of-xbox/)).

Tom's Hardware described the original Xbox as running an Intel Pentium III 733 MHz CPU and an Nvidia NV2A 233 MHz GPU, with 64MB of DDR SDRAM, an 8GB hard drive and an Ethernet jack ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/the-original-xbox-was-released-25-years-ago-today-microsofts-pentium-iii-and-nvidia-gpu-powered-console-did-surprisingly-well-in-a-ps2-dominated-era)). Xbox Live launched in 2002 ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/the-original-xbox-was-released-25-years-ago-today-microsofts-pentium-iii-and-nvidia-gpu-powered-console-did-surprisingly-well-in-a-ps2-dominated-era)).

The bundle titles include Skyrim Special Edition, Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary, Gears of War: Reloaded and Fallout 76 ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/the-original-xbox-was-released-25-years-ago-today-microsofts-pentium-iii-and-nvidia-gpu-powered-console-did-surprisingly-well-in-a-ps2-dominated-era)). Buyers of qualifying machines find them in the Microsoft Store Library under the Included with device tab ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/the-original-xbox-was-released-25-years-ago-today-microsofts-pentium-iii-and-nvidia-gpu-powered-console-did-surprisingly-well-in-a-ps2-dominated-era)). Free wallpapers, in-game anniversary items and an Xbox account badge are listed on the anniversary page ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/the-original-xbox-was-released-25-years-ago-today-microsofts-pentium-iii-and-nvidia-gpu-powered-console-did-surprisingly-well-in-a-ps2-dominated-era)).
