Wired reported on 2026-11-07 that Netgear, Eero and Asus, among others, have secured a Conditional Approval from the FCC under the regulator's foreign-made router ban, announced in March ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)). TP-Link has so far been unsuccessful in its attempts to win the same approval in the US ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)).

Routers already on the market can continue to be sold ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)).

## Timeline

- **2026-11-07**: Wired updated its router guide, adding the Asus ROG Rapture BN98 Pro as its best Wi-Fi 8 router and the TP-Link Archer 8 Ultra to its list of other routers it tested and liked ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)).
- **2029-01-01**: Existing routers can be updated with new firmware at least until this date ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Wired described the Archer 8 Ultra as a tri-band Wi-Fi 8 tower priced at £900 and tested in the UK, where it was a very solid performer offering stable and expansive Wi-Fi ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)). The reviewer found the Asus ROG Rapture BN98 Pro, listed at $900, better and cheaper than the TP-Link model ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)). Wired also noted that the TP-Link model needs subscriptions to unlock full parental controls and security features ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)).

Wired named the Asus RT-BE58U its best router for most people, with an MSRP of $145 ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)). Its best budget pick is the TP-Link Archer BE230, listed at $120 ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/gallery/best-wifi-routers/)).

Tom's Hardware reported that the TP-Link Archer BE3600, also known as the BE230, fell to $79.98, the lowest price it saw over Amazon's sales event last month ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/routers/this-usd79-98-tp-link-wireless-router-with-wi-fi-7-tech-is-back-down-to-its-lowest-ever-price-archer-be3600-unlocks-multi-gigabit-networking-ideal-for-gaming-or-4k-streaming-with-33-percent-off)). The outlet noted that the router lacks the 6 GHz band and so does not support the full Wi-Fi 7 spec ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/routers/this-usd79-98-tp-link-wireless-router-with-wi-fi-7-tech-is-back-down-to-its-lowest-ever-price-archer-be3600-unlocks-multi-gigabit-networking-ideal-for-gaming-or-4k-streaming-with-33-percent-off)). It lists one 2.5 Gbps WAN port, one extra 2.5 Gbps port and three 1 Gbps ports ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/routers/this-usd79-98-tp-link-wireless-router-with-wi-fi-7-tech-is-back-down-to-its-lowest-ever-price-archer-be3600-unlocks-multi-gigabit-networking-ideal-for-gaming-or-4k-streaming-with-33-percent-off)).
