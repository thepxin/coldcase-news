Google announced the winners of its annual Best of Play awards on November 17, 2026, naming Arknights: Endfield the best game and Notability: AI & PDF Notes the best app ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)). The company also chose Moonlight Peaks as the best cross-device game and Strava: Run, Bike, Walk as the best cross-device app ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)).

## Details (as Reported)

In its announcement, Google described the awards as a way to put the work of developers and publishers front and center, both on and off the store ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)). It wrote that in games, studios continue to weave rich narratives into core gameplay, but at a larger scale, with optimizations that let players jump between phones, tablets, and PCs ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)).

The company described Arknights: Endfield as a game with slick real-time combat, rich characters, and deep factory-building and management mechanics ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)). Its write-up adds that every inch of the game has the awards team hooked, from its story to its vibrant vistas ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)).

For Moonlight Peaks, Google cited tending to magical crops and wooing werewolves, along with catching up on town gossip on the go ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)). The company added that longer sessions can be played with Google Play Games on PC or on Googlebook ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)).

Subway Surfers City is listed for Best Pick Up & Play ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)). Google lists the full set of U.S. winners in its post, and it points readers to the Best of 2026 page on Play and to local winners in Asia Pacific ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-of-play-awards-2026/)).
