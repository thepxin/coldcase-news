Google announced on November 11, 2026 that the Gemini app is coming to Apple CarPlay, bringing Gemini Live conversations to the car display for iOS users ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)). The rollout is global and starts that day for all Gemini users on iOS across every supported Gemini Live language ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)). Engadget reported that Google had previously offered the assistant on CarPlay through the Google Maps app ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2314093/google-brings-the-gemini-app-to-apple-carplay/)). 9to5Mac reported that Gemini was the last major AI chatbot holdout on CarPlay, noting that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Meta AI and Grok had already shipped CarPlay support ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/11/google-confirms-gemini-app-coming-to-apple-carplay/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Google described the product as a conversational driving companion designed to minimize distraction ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)). Users tap the Gemini icon on the CarPlay screen or side rail, then pick up a recent chat or start a fresh conversation ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)). Conversations run at the driver's own pace, with room to interrupt, change the subject or pause ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)).

9to5Google described a simple waveform interface that shows when Gemini is listening, thinking and responding, with End and Mute buttons in the top-left corner ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/11/gemini-app-carplay/)).

Google listed four uses: preparing for the day ahead, talking through an inbox, exploring local spots and landmarks, and brainstorming or rehearsing out loud ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)). The company stated that Gemini connects with Google Workspace apps such as Gmail and Google Calendar, which allows a daily brief, unread email catch-ups and summaries of long email threads by voice ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)).

Google noted that the CarPlay launch follows its rollout on Android Auto and cars with Google built-in ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/)). Engadget added that Google brought the app to CarPlay not long after enabling personal features in the chatbot on Android Auto, and that Personal Intelligence is also coming to Gemini on cars with Google built-in ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2314093/google-brings-the-gemini-app-to-apple-carplay/)).
