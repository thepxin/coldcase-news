Rockstar Games will release Grand Theft Auto VI on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, according to Take-Two Interactive ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-3)). In February 2025, Take-Two listed the game for "Fall of calendar 2025" ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-third-3)), then gave a launch date of May 26, 2026 when Rockstar released the second trailer ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/adding-multimedia-rockstar-games-releases-trailer-2-grand-theft)). Engadget reported in June 2026 that the game "has already been delayed twice" ([Engadget](https://engadget.com/2200483/rockstar-reveals-gta-6-pricing-launch/)).

Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, with the standard game at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99 ([Take-Two](https://ir.take2games.com/node/32311/pdf)). In August, Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick pointed to "excitement around the November 19th launch of Grand Theft Auto VI" ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-first-6)).

## Timeline
- **2025-02-06**: Take-Two listed Grand Theft Auto VI for "Fall of calendar 2025" on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-third-3)).
- **2025-05-06**: Rockstar released the second trailer, with a launch date of May 26, 2026 ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/adding-multimedia-rockstar-games-releases-trailer-2-grand-theft)).
- **2025-11-06**: Take-Two announced the game is "now launching November 19, 2026" ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-3)).
- **2026-06-24**: Rockstar announced pre-orders from June 25 at $79.99, with digital pre-loading from November 12 ([Take-Two](https://ir.take2games.com/node/32311/pdf)).
- **2026-06-24**: Engadget reported that the game "has already been delayed twice" ([Engadget](https://engadget.com/2200483/rockstar-reveals-gta-6-pricing-launch/)).
- **2026-08-07**: Take-Two repeated the November 19 launch and kept its Fiscal 2027 Net Bookings outlook of $8.0 to $8.2 billion ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-first-6)).

## Details (as Reported)
Announcing the November date in 2025, Strauss Zelnick said Take-Two remains "both excited and confident they will deliver an unrivalled blockbuster entertainment experience" ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-3)). Every pre-order and purchase made before November 20, 2026 includes the Vintage Vice City Pack, according to Rockstar ([Take-Two](https://ir.take2games.com/node/32311/pdf)). Players who pre-order a digital copy can begin pre-loading on November 12, 2026 ([Take-Two](https://ir.take2games.com/node/32311/pdf)).

In a job listing, Rockstar described the game as "the largest game launch in history", Engadget reported ([Engadget](https://engadget.com/2200483/rockstar-reveals-gta-6-pricing-launch/)). In August 2026, Zelnick put the outlook in one sentence: "With these positive trends and excitement around the November 19th launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, we are reiterating our Fiscal 2027 Net Bookings outlook of $8.0 to $8.2 billion" ([Take-Two](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-first-6)).
