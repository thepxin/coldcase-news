PlugShare released version 8.0 of its EV charging app for iPhone worldwide on November 10, adding dark mode, a trip planner that shows the distance between stops, and a rebuilt Apple Watch app ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)). The release is described as a big overhaul of the app ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)). The update is rolling out through the App Store ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Dark mode is one of the most-requested features from PlugShare users ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)). When an iPhone switches to dark mode at sunset, the charging map follows ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)).

Under the new trip planner, each charging stop shows its distance from both the start of the trip and the previous stop ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)). On opening, or when a user signs in, the app now loads saved locations, trips and the profile ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)). Station photos open and zoom from their thumbnails, and the layout adapts to the different screens and folded positions of the iPhone Duo ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)).

Filters set on the iPhone carry over to the rebuilt Apple Watch app, so a filter for plugs that fit a driver's EV reaches the wrist ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)). On the watch, a tap on a station brings up its PlugScore, the network that runs it, a small map and its plugs, including NACS, CCS, CHAdeMO or J1772 ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)). Lists refresh when the wrist is raised and the app is opened, and a shortcut can go into a round or corner slot on the watch face ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/plugshare-finally-adds-dark-mode-and-theres-more-for-ev-drivers/)).

The App Store listing calls PlugShare the world's largest EV driver community, where drivers contribute station reviews and photos ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/plugshare-charging-stations/id421788217)). Each station's PlugScore rating is built on real driver reviews, according to the listing ([Apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/plugshare-charging-stations/id421788217)).
