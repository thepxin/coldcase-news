People who received the new Apple TV 4K on November 13 saw error messages as soon as they opened the YouTube app, The Verge reported ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1033076/apple-tv-4k-youtube-error)). The first units initially showed a black screen with a QR code and the message "YouTube is not yet available on this device" ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1033076/apple-tv-4k-youtube-error)). 9to5Mac reported that YouTube has started rolling out a server-side fix ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/new-apple-tv-youtube-broken-fix/)).

## Details (as Reported)

In some cases the app was functional, but a transparent red bar stayed at the bottom of the screen with the message "YouTube app is approved for device testing only and not for production use" ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1033076/apple-tv-4k-youtube-error)).

9to5Mac called the problem "pretty widespread" and reported that a number of new Apple TV 4K users complained on social media and Reddit ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/new-apple-tv-youtube-broken-fix/)). Its list of steps begins with making sure the Apple TV is updated to tvOS 27.2 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/new-apple-tv-youtube-broken-fix/)). The restart can be done by disconnecting the device from the power outlet, by choosing Restart under Settings and then System, or by holding the TV and back buttons for five seconds ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/new-apple-tv-youtube-broken-fix/)). The last two steps are to uninstall the YouTube app and reinstall it from the App Store ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/new-apple-tv-youtube-broken-fix/)).

The Verge reported that installing tvOS 27.2 and rebooting was not enough to resolve the issue for its writer Tom Warren, who also had to uninstall and reinstall the app ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1033076/apple-tv-4k-youtube-error)). In his review of the new Apple TV 4K, The Verge's John Higgins called it "one of the best streaming devices available," but expensive compared to other streaming boxes ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1033076/apple-tv-4k-youtube-error)).
