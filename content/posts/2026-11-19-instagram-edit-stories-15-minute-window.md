Instagram head Adam Mosseri has announced that users can edit their Stories for up to 15 minutes after sharing them, without losing views, likes or replies ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036941/instagram-now-lets-you-edit-your-stories-after-you-post-them)). The editable elements are text, music, stickers and drawings ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036941/instagram-now-lets-you-edit-your-stories-after-you-post-them)). Engadget reports that the feature is rolling out now ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320953/you-can-finally-edit-an-instagram-story-without-deleting-it/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Previously, users had to delete and repost a Story to fix a typo or add a song they had forgotten ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036941/instagram-now-lets-you-edit-your-stories-after-you-post-them)). The Verge reports that the reverse engineer Alessandro Paluzzi first spotted Instagram working on the feature in April ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036941/instagram-now-lets-you-edit-your-stories-after-you-post-them)).

According to The Verge, users open a Story, select the three-dot menu in the bottom-right corner and tap "Edit Story" ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036941/instagram-now-lets-you-edit-your-stories-after-you-post-them)). Engadget describes the route as the "more" menu followed by "edit story" ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320953/you-can-finally-edit-an-instagram-story-without-deleting-it/)).

Engadget notes that Stories slides appear in reverse chronological order, so a deleted and re-shared Story cannot return to the same place when other images sit immediately before and after it ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320953/you-can-finally-edit-an-instagram-story-without-deleting-it/)). It adds that professional creators and social media managers may also worry about a do-over affecting their metrics ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320953/you-can-finally-edit-an-instagram-story-without-deleting-it/)). Meta has long allowed users to edit regular Instagram posts after the fact ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320953/you-can-finally-edit-an-instagram-story-without-deleting-it/)).

Mosseri wrote, "A lot of people have been asking for this, so I'm excited to get this one out there" ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320953/you-can-finally-edit-an-instagram-story-without-deleting-it/)).

Instagram also announced new fonts, colors and shapes for stickers, presented on sticker sheets designed to be easier to browse ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036941/instagram-now-lets-you-edit-your-stories-after-you-post-them)). The platform is changing how users select multiple photos and adding new blur, color and gradient background options ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1036941/instagram-now-lets-you-edit-your-stories-after-you-post-them)). Engadget describes the same changes as easier sharing and formatting of several images at once ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320953/you-can-finally-edit-an-instagram-story-without-deleting-it/)).
