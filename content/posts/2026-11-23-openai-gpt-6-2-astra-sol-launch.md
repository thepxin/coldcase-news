OpenAI introduced GPT-6.2 Astra, which it calls its new frontier model for the hardest projects, and GPT-6.2 Sol, its efficient, versatile model for everyday work, on 2026-11-17 ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). Both models are open to all Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, 9to5Google reported ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/17/openai-releases-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-for-complex-projects-a-week-after-claude-5-2/)). The launch arrived a week after Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/17/openai-releases-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-for-complex-projects-a-week-after-claude-5-2/)).

## Details (as Reported)

In the API, OpenAI priced GPT-6.2 Astra input and output tokens 20% lower than GPT-6 Astra, with a 60% discount on cached input ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). GPT-6.2 Sol keeps the API token prices of GPT-6.1 Sol ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). Those Sol prices are listed by 9to5Google as $2 for input, $0.10 for cached input and $10 for output, per million tokens ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/17/openai-releases-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-for-complex-projects-a-week-after-claude-5-2/)).

Agents' Last Exam results put GPT-6.2 Astra 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra at less than a third of the cost, and 5.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at 5% of the cost ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 gave the company a score 9 percentage points higher than GPT-6 Astra, at less than one-third the cost ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). GPT-6.2 Astra led the models tested on its internal GDPval v2 benchmark, up 6.2 percentage points from GPT-6 Astra while using 22% fewer output tokens ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). Artificial Analysis's AA-3D ranked GPT-6.2 Astra first with 1,318 Elo, followed by Claude Fable 5.2 at 1,271 and GPT-6.2 Sol at 1,259 ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)).

OpenAI describes Sol as smarter and more efficient than GPT-6.1 Sol, and as able to complete more tasks on the same paid plan ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). Sol comes within 0.5 points of Astra on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 at about one-fifth the cost, with both at max ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)). On its LifeVal evaluation, Sol outscores GPT-6 Astra at about one-seventh the cost ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol)).

Anthropic announced Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on 2026-11-09 at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). Its benchmark table lists Fable 5.2 at 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 at Xhigh effort and 74.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). 9to5Google reported that OpenAI shows the new models playing ball with Anthropic's latest models, often at a drastically lower cost ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/17/openai-releases-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-for-complex-projects-a-week-after-claude-5-2/)).
