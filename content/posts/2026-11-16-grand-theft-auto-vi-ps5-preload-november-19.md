PlayStation announced on November 10 that pre-loading of Grand Theft Auto VI is open on PS5 at PlayStation Store, nine days before the game's scheduled launch on November 19 ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). The company has adopted a rolling midnight launch by country, beginning at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand and running across the globe as each country reaches midnight on November 19 ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). It also described the title as the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)).

## Details (as Reported)

PlayStation said it joined with Rockstar Games to allow players to start pre-loading nine days early, so they are ready to play on launch day ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). The company strongly advises that everyone hoping to play on launch day pre-load as soon as possible ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). Players who bought the code-in-box version can activate their code as soon as they receive it to begin pre-loading ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). Those who have not yet pre-ordered are urged to do so to be ready for November 19 ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)).

On PS5, the company states that Rockstar developed the game from the ground up to harness the console's power ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). The game uses the DualSense wireless controller's integrated speakers, haptics and adaptive triggers, which are adjustable via accessibility settings ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). In-game sounds take advantage of Tempest 3D Audio, and PlayStation states that load times are improved by the PS5's ultra high-speed SSD ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). PlayStation also claims the game plays best on PlayStation 5 consoles ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)).

PS5 Pro owners get further enhancements, which PlayStation lists as improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)). The announcement also links a video showcasing unique PS5 features for the game ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)).
