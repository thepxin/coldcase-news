OpenAI published a customer story on November 12, 2026, in which Amplitude estimated that its Wave product agent can help teams ship product improvements in 1-2 days, down from roughly a month ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). Amplitude describes Wave as roughly 80% powered by OpenAI models, including GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Luna and GPT-Image-2.5 Flare ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). The company calls Wave a proactive product agent that identifies what to build, hands work to coding agents and measures the impact ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)).

## Details (as Reported)

Wave then brings together Amplitude data, session replays, customer feedback and code to identify and prioritize opportunities ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). When an opportunity is ready, teams can send it to coding agents, including Codex, with a technical plan and acceptance criteria ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). Amplitude's own page describes the loop as designing new builds, orchestrating coding agents to generate pull requests and running experiments to validate impact ([Amplitude (official statement)](https://www.amplitude.com/wave)).

In a sample of 100 session replays, 80 users typed their own questions and only 12 selected a suggestion card ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). Wave proposed personalizing the cards, and after a brief review the team handed the work to a coding agent and A/B tested the change ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). In the readout Amplitude shared, messages sent through the cards rose 160% relative to control after one day ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)).

For the Opportunity Investigator, GPT-6.1 Sol scored 4.06 out of 5 overall, against 3.62, 3.52 and 2.94 for three alternative models, and had the fastest median investigation time at 54 minutes ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). In the Data Science Agent evaluation, GPT-6.1 Sol scored 67.5 out of 100 against 69.3 for another leading model, at a mean model cost of $1.28 versus $2.63 per dispatch ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). GPT-6 Luna handles frequent, lower-cost tasks such as scanning code and running small evaluation checks ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). For solution wireframes, OpenAI's image model scored 80.3 out of 100 against 63.9 for another image model across 15 opportunities ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)).

Amplitude said most teams send bug fixes straight to coding agents, while new features and bigger bets get a human review ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). Among customer-launched experiments, flags and guides, one in four went live the same day Wave surfaced the opportunity, half in under four days and two in three within a week ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/amplitude)). Amplitude's page cites a PixAI case in which an out-of-credits tooltip led to publishing rising nearly 10% and Day 1 retention rising 3% over control ([Amplitude (official statement)](https://www.amplitude.com/wave)).

Amplitude lists requirements for Wave: an eligible Growth or Enterprise plan, meaningful Product Analytics and Session Replay data, and a business goal to improve ([Amplitude (official statement)](https://www.amplitude.com/wave)). It also states that connected code is not used to train shared models ([Amplitude (official statement)](https://www.amplitude.com/wave)).
