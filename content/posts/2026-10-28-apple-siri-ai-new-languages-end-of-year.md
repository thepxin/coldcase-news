Apple released iOS 27.2 and the other 27.2 software updates on October 26, 2026, extending Siri AI to French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)). The company announced that Chinese (traditional), Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese will follow by the end of the year ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)). Siri AI is in beta in the new release and requires an Apple Intelligence-enabled device set to a supported language ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)). Before the update, Siri AI was available in English only, according to 9to5Mac ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-announces-siri-ai-will-expand-to-these-new-languages-soon/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Apple describes Siri AI as a completely reimagined version of Siri, built on Apple Intelligence, that draws on personal context across messages, emails and photos ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)). The release notes list support on all iPhone 16 models and later, iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro Max ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-releases-ios-27-2-heres-whats-new/)).

The iOS 27.2 notes add that Siri AI can recall past conversations and draw on photos attached in Messages, Calendar event attendees, voicemails, Safari history and Reading List ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-releases-ios-27-2-heres-whats-new/)). An Excluded Apps setting lets users choose which apps Siri, Search and suggestions can access ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-releases-ios-27-2-heres-whats-new/)).

Apple stated that Siri AI is not available for users under 13 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)). Certain server-side features, including Siri AI, are subject to daily usage limits that may vary by feature, request complexity and system demand ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)). Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future, Apple added ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- Apple has not named the update that will carry the four remaining languages, while 9to5Mac reported that iOS 27.3 is expected to ship in December ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-announces-siri-ai-will-expand-to-these-new-languages-soon/)).
- Apple said it is working constructively with the EU in an attempt to find a path forward for Siri AI, and no date is given ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)).
- Bloomberg reported that Apple plans to launch some initial Apple Intelligence features in China with iOS 27.3, and that Siri AI is not expected to be among them ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/26/apple-announces-siri-ai-will-expand-to-these-new-languages-soon/)).
- Apple did not state the price or date for expanded access to features with daily usage limits ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/siri-ai-is-available-in-more-languages-starting-today/)).
