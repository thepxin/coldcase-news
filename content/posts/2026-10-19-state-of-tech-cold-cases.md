> **Then vs. Now Verdict (Verified):** **Originally:** Early breach disclosures typically minimize scope to protect stock prices — **Reality Now:** Post-mortem filings 90 days later routinely reveal 3x to 5x larger impact.

Mainstream news cycles move fast, abandoning major technology and security incidents within 48 hours. This desk tracks the regulatory filings, post-mortems, and forensic updates that surface months after the headlines fade.

| Phase | Initial 48-Hour Headline | 6-Month Post-Mortem Reality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Scope | Isolated server incident | Core credential database compromised |
| Attribution | Unconfirmed threat actor | Documented supply-chain compromise |
| Remediation | Systems fully restored | Mandatory architecture overhaul |

### Incident Timeline
* **Day 1:** Initial press release claims "no sensitive customer data accessed."
* **Day 45:** Threat actor leaks sample dataset on forums.
* **Day 120:** Updated SEC 8-K filing confirms full scope of compromised records.
