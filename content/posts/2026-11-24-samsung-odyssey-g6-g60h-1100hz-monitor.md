Samsung announced the 27-inch Odyssey G6 G60H gaming monitor, which Engadget describes as the world's first 1,100Hz gaming monitor ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325384/samsung-odyssey-g6-g60h-is-the-worlds-first-1100hz-gaming-monitor/)). The panel reaches that figure only in HD mode, and Engadget reports that it cannot offer its maximum resolution and maximum refresh rate at the same time ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325384/samsung-odyssey-g6-g60h-is-the-worlds-first-1100hz-gaming-monitor/)). Samsung lists the refresh rate as a maximum of 600 Hz at QHD and 1100 Hz at HD ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/ca/monitors/gaming/odyssey-g6-g60h-27-inch-dual-mode-qhd-600hz-hd-1100hz-ls27hg602enxza/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Engadget reports that the monitor uses a dual mode IPS panel with 400 nits typical brightness, HDR10+ certification and a 1ms gray-to-gray response time ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325384/samsung-odyssey-g6-g60h-is-the-worlds-first-1100hz-gaming-monitor/)). It also carries Samsung's Odyssey Clear Motion technology, which uses a custom algorithm to reduce blur, and a black equalizer feature that adjusts brightness in dark scenes ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325384/samsung-odyssey-g6-g60h-is-the-worlds-first-1100hz-gaming-monitor/)).

Samsung's specification list includes one DisplayPort 2.1 input, two HDMI 2.1 inputs, a Virtual Aim Point crosshair and Auto Source Switch+ ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/ca/monitors/gaming/odyssey-g6-g60h-27-inch-dual-mode-qhd-600hz-hd-1100hz-ls27hg602enxza/)). Engadget notes that Auto Source Switch+ detects new inputs as soon as they are plugged in ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325384/samsung-odyssey-g6-g60h-is-the-worlds-first-1100hz-gaming-monitor/)).

Dual Mode and the native 600Hz maximum are not supported on macOS devices ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/ca/monitors/gaming/odyssey-g6-g60h-27-inch-dual-mode-qhd-600hz-hd-1100hz-ls27hg602enxza/)). AMD FreeSync Premium does not support 1100Hz, and AMD Radeon RX6 Series cards and below do not support 1000Hz or higher ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/ca/monitors/gaming/odyssey-g6-g60h-27-inch-dual-mode-qhd-600hz-hd-1100hz-ls27hg602enxza/)). The 1ms figure is based on internal test conditions and requires the Extreme mode setting ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/ca/monitors/gaming/odyssey-g6-g60h-27-inch-dual-mode-qhd-600hz-hd-1100hz-ls27hg602enxza/)).

Engadget says that for pro-level FPS players, the panel could deliver a little bit of extra competitive advantage ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325384/samsung-odyssey-g6-g60h-is-the-worlds-first-1100hz-gaming-monitor/)). It adds that even in QHD mode the panel can reach 600Hz ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325384/samsung-odyssey-g6-g60h-is-the-worlds-first-1100hz-gaming-monitor/)).
