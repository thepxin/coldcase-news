Apple added UFC coverage to its free Apple Sports app on 2026-11-12, giving MMA fans live stats for each bout on a fight card ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-sports-adds-ufc-events-with-real-time-stats-and-results/)). Engadget reported that the live updates start with this Saturday's UFC 334 card ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2313383/apple-sports-adds-live-ufc-updates/)).

The app covers every bout across the full fight card, with live stats such as total strike counts, takedowns and knockdowns ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-sports-adds-ufc-events-with-real-time-stats-and-results/)). UFC 334 features two title fights: Ciryl Gane defends the Heavyweight Championship against Josh Hokit, and Amanda Nunes challenges Kayla Harrison for the Women's Bantamweight Championship ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2313383/apple-sports-adds-live-ufc-updates/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Engadget reported that fans who cannot watch UFC 334 or future events on Paramount+ can follow the action through "enhanced data visualizations and dynamic on-screen animations" in Apple Sports ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2313383/apple-sports-adds-live-ufc-updates/)). Before each bout, including prelims, the app shows the tale of the tape and fighter rankings ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2313383/apple-sports-adds-live-ufc-updates/)).

Once fights are underway, the app delivers real-time updates on total strikes, significant strikes, takedowns, knockdowns, submission attempts and control time ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2313383/apple-sports-adds-live-ufc-updates/)). 9to5Mac reported that the detail view uses animated background artwork, and that UFC gets a custom backdrop evoking the ambience of being under the lights of the Octagon ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-sports-adds-ufc-events-with-real-time-stats-and-results/)). The details card also includes a new visualization of the number of recorded strikes by target ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-sports-adds-ufc-events-with-real-time-stats-and-results/)).

Users can tap the clock icon to start a Live Activity and receive score updates on the iPhone lock screen ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-sports-adds-ufc-events-with-real-time-stats-and-results/)). Apple Sports now comes preinstalled when an iPhone is set up from scratch ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-sports-adds-ufc-events-with-real-time-stats-and-results/)).

Engadget reported that the app debuted in February 2024 with basketball, hockey and soccer, and that football and tennis have joined since ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2313383/apple-sports-adds-live-ufc-updates/)). Apple also brought it to many more markets ahead of the men's World Cup earlier this year ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2313383/apple-sports-adds-live-ufc-updates/)).
