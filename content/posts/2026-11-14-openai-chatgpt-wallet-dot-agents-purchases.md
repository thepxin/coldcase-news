OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on November 13, a payment feature that lets its dot agents help users decide what to buy and then complete the purchase ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)). The company says agents pay without access to users' actual card details, and that every purchase requires a security check ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-enabling-dot-agents-to-make-purchases/)). It is available first to a limited number of US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Users add an eligible card to the wallet once, then ask their dot to help with a purchase ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)). Before checkout, the user reviews the items, the total and the selected card, then approves with a security check, and each approval creates a record of the purchase the user authorized ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-enabling-dot-agents-to-make-purchases/)).

After approval, the payment network provides a payment token with a card number, security code and expiration date, and the selected card is charged without the actual card details reaching the business or the agent ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-enabling-dot-agents-to-make-purchases/)). The Verge reports that OpenAI likens this to tap to pay, Apple Pay or Google Wallet ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033425/openai-chatgpt-wallet)). The merchant receives details such as the shipping address and contact information from the dot, and OpenAI states that cardholders keep card rewards and dispute protections ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)).

OpenAI has partnered with American Express, Mastercard and Visa for the feature ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2316137/you-can-now-tempt-fate-and-give-your-banking-information-to-chatgpt/)). Chaya Nayak, who leads product for monetization and payments at OpenAI, told CNBC the goal is to focus on merchants and network partners and build a foundation that is safe and easy to use ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2316137/you-can-now-tempt-fate-and-give-your-banking-information-to-chatgpt/)). OpenAI also published a Commerce Partner Playbook with tools and guidance for sites that want to support customers using agents ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)).

The feature will not work with websites that block agents ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-enabling-dot-agents-to-make-purchases/)). Engadget reports that Delta and United Airlines ban agents from making purchases ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2316137/you-can-now-tempt-fate-and-give-your-banking-information-to-chatgpt/)). TechCrunch identifies Amazon as the largest retailer to ban AI agents ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- OpenAI expects users to be able to delegate purchases entirely to their agent by setting parameters such as spending limits, purchase categories or time periods, but no date is given ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033425/openai-chatgpt-wallet)).
- OpenAI plans to work with businesses, banks and payment networks to expand availability beyond the limited launch, and no timing is given ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2316137/you-can-now-tempt-fate-and-give-your-banking-information-to-chatgpt/)).
- OpenAI states that websites need to ensure agents are not inadvertently blocked, and that payment and risk systems need to recognize legitimate purchases made by agents ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033425/openai-chatgpt-wallet)).
