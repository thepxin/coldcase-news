Jensen Huang, the chief executive officer of NVIDIA, told employees at an all-hands meeting on November 24, 2026, that he was grateful for a second chance at the company, TechCrunch reported ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/in-all-hands-meeting-jensen-huang-thanks-white-house-and-says-hes-in-therapy/)). The meeting followed the company's announcement on November 23, 2026, that the board had voted unanimously to reinstate him as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, effective immediately ([NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/)).

According to the same report, Huang also told staff that he is in therapy ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/in-all-hands-meeting-jensen-huang-thanks-white-house-and-says-hes-in-therapy/)). He thanked the administration for providing the company with quick access to intelligence, which he said helped the board judge that the national security risks of reinstating him were low ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/in-all-hands-meeting-jensen-huang-thanks-white-house-and-says-hes-in-therapy/)).

TechCrunch also reported that Huang is scheduled to appear at a public hearing of the Senate Banking Committee the following week, and that he will be called before closed hearings of the House Intelligence Committee, Foreign Affairs Committee and China Select Committee in the coming weeks ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/in-all-hands-meeting-jensen-huang-thanks-white-house-and-says-hes-in-therapy/)).
