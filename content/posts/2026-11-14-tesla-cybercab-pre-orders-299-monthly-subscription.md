Tesla invited select customers on Friday evening to pre-order its autonomous Cybercab for personal use, with an estimated delivery date of December 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/tesla-opens-cybercab-pre-orders-for-personal-use-with-geo-restrictions-and-a-299-monthly-fee/)). The order page lists the car at $29,990 before taxes, with delivery estimated in Austin, Texas, and a $1,000 non-refundable deposit ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-cybercab-personal-use-orders-299-subscription-geofence/)). Tesla's agreement states that the car will not operate without an active $299 monthly Autonomy Subscription ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/14/tesla-cybercab-autonomy-subscription-agreement-terms/)).

## Details (as Reported)

- Tech reviewer Marques Brownlee posted that buyers must accept delivery in one of a handful of Texas cities and that the Cybercab will operate only in certain geofenced areas ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/tesla-opens-cybercab-pre-orders-for-personal-use-with-geo-restrictions-and-a-299-monthly-fee/)).
- Tesla's Ashok Elluswamy said the Cybercab will work unsupervised inside all the same geo locations as the Robotaxi service, serving the owners' needs ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/tesla-opens-cybercab-pre-orders-for-personal-use-with-geo-restrictions-and-a-299-monthly-fee/)).
- Electrek reported that Tesla runs its Robotaxi service in Austin, Dallas, Houston, Fort Worth and San Antonio in Texas, and in Miami, Orlando and Tampa in Florida ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-cybercab-personal-use-orders-299-subscription-geofence/)).
- The car has two seats, a 21-inch center touchscreen, 20.1 cubic feet of cargo space, and no steering wheel or pedals ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-cybercab-personal-use-orders-299-subscription-geofence/)).
- According to Electrek, the agreement lets Tesla change the subscription price with one month's notice and modify the operating area at any time ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/14/tesla-cybercab-autonomy-subscription-agreement-terms/)).
- Electrek also reported that three or more strikeouts for improper or unsafe use trigger a one-week suspension of autonomous driving, without proration of payment ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/14/tesla-cybercab-autonomy-subscription-agreement-terms/)).
- The agreement bars use on Tesla's Robotaxi network, Uber, a delivery app or a rental platform without Tesla's written authorization, according to Electrek ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/14/tesla-cybercab-autonomy-subscription-agreement-terms/)).
- Electrek reported that Tesla collects Autonomy Data, including cabin camera image data kept for 30 days by default ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/14/tesla-cybercab-autonomy-subscription-agreement-terms/)).
- Independent tracker Koby Pierce found that the areas where Tesla accepts pickups and drop-offs are 59% to 78% of the advertised geofences in the six cities he checked, according to Electrek ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-cybercab-personal-use-orders-299-subscription-geofence/)).
- After Tesla fell far short of projected sales, it announced it will cease Cybertruck production next year and use the capacity to build more Cybercabs ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/tesla-opens-cybercab-pre-orders-for-personal-use-with-geo-restrictions-and-a-299-monthly-fee/)).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- NHTSA has not ruled on Tesla's October 30 response about the car's lack of a brake pedal, according to Electrek ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-cybercab-personal-use-orders-299-subscription-geofence/)).
- Elon Musk said the Cybercab will be available nationwide as Tesla receives regulatory approval ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/tesla-opens-cybercab-pre-orders-for-personal-use-with-geo-restrictions-and-a-299-monthly-fee/)).
- Musk said the $299 monthly fee is too high and should be more like $115 a month ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/tesla-opens-cybercab-pre-orders-for-personal-use-with-geo-restrictions-and-a-299-monthly-fee/)).
- Electrek reported that Elluswamy said on Tesla's Q3 2026 earnings call that customer cars might join the network around Q2 2027 ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/14/tesla-cybercab-autonomy-subscription-agreement-terms/)).
