NVIDIA announced the Vera CPU rack-scale platform for high-performance computing on November 16, 2026, and expects Vera CPU racks to be available from Dell Technologies, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro beginning in early 2027 ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). The platform pairs 192 Vera CPUs with NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand networking and is aimed at climate modeling, molecular dynamics and other scientific workloads ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). The chipmaker describes Vera as its first fully custom data center CPU ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)).

## Details (as Reported)

Each Vera CPU has 88 custom NVIDIA Olympus cores and 176 threads, with SOCAMM2 LPDDR5X memory delivering up to 1.2TB/s of bandwidth, according to NVIDIA ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). The rack combines 192 Vera CPUs with approximately 300TB of memory and 1 petabyte of storage in a fully liquid-cooled NVIDIA MGX design ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). Each 1U tray integrates eight Vera CPUs, and NVMe storage and management ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)).

The liquid-cooled system delivers more than 200TB/s of aggregate peak memory bandwidth, the company states ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). The platform reuses NVL72 rack components, including chassis, power distribution and cooling infrastructure ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). NVIDIA SHARP In-Network Computing is meant to reduce the time processors spend coordinating their work ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)).

In NVIDIA's tests, Vera delivered 1.9x the performance and 2.4x the performance per watt of x86 systems on OpenFOAM's motorBike Large airflow simulation ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). NVIDIA has also benchmarked the open source Weather Research and Forecasting model on Vera CPUs, with projected performance gains of 1.6x and energy-efficiency gains of 2x compared with x86 ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)).

DKRZ, the German Climate Computing Center, selected Vera CPUs for its HLRE-5 supercomputer, alongside NVIDIA GB200 Grace Blackwell systems and NVIDIA Quantum-2 InfiniBand networking ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). The system will support climate simulation and AI research, enabling global kilometer-scale climate simulations with lower energy consumption than on the current system, according to the announcement ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)).

Ian Buck, NVIDIA's vice president of hyperscale and high-performance computing, said that with compilers and libraries optimized for Vera, researchers can run demanding simulations more efficiently ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)). NVIDIA directs readers to its Vera CPU HPC performance brief for detailed benchmark results and test configurations ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-expands-vera-cpu-platform-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery)).
