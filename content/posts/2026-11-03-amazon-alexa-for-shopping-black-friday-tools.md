Amazon detailed on October 30 how its Alexa for Shopping assistant can track prices and set deal alerts ahead of Black Friday, which falls on November 27, 2026 ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)). The company describes the tool as an AI shopping assistant that surfaces relevant deals, tracks prices and keeps customers informed ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)). Early deals are available in the Holiday Shop now ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)).

## Details (as Reported)

Amazon said Alexa for Shopping can monitor items a customer is watching, and customers can set up auto-buy to complete the purchase automatically when the price hits their target ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)). Tapping Price History on a product page, or asking the assistant, shows up to 365 days of pricing data ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)).

The assistant can also notify customers when favorite brands release something new ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)). Customers can create and share Wish Lists, organize a registry and move items between lists by voice ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)). Amazon listed more than 100 everyday shopping actions, including adjusting Subscribe & Save deliveries, checking a gift card balance, tracking refunds and managing Prime and Audible memberships ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)).

With enhanced Alexa+ deal tracking, customers can get instant notifications when watched items go on sale ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)).

On membership, Amazon priced Prime at $14.99 per month or $139 per year, with a free 30-day trial for eligible customers ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)). Prime for Young Adults, for ages 18 to 24 and college students, starts with a 6-month $0 trial and then costs $7.49 per month or $69 per year ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)). Prime Access, for qualifying government-assistance recipients and income-verified customers, offers 30 days before a charge of $6.99 per month ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-amazon-black-friday-faq?utm_source=rss)).
