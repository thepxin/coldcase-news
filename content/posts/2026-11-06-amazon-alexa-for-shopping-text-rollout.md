Amazon on 2026-11-06 began rolling out texting with its Alexa for Shopping agent, starting with a small group of U.S. customers by invitation only ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)). The company announced that the option will expand to more customers over the coming weeks ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)). Selected customers receive a text message asking them to enable the feature ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/after-blocking-muse-amazon-now-lets-users-text-alexa-to-help-them-shop/)).

## Timeline

## Details (as Reported)

Amazon describes Alexa for Shopping as an agent that takes action on customers' behalf, including ordering weekly groceries, adding recipe ingredients to a cart, looking for deals and texting when an item hits a target price ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)). To start, customers accept the terms, link a phone number and begin shopping from their text messages ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)).

According to Amazon, a text can ask the agent to compare products, check up to a full year of price history, track price drops, manage lists and report on order status ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)). Customers can also send a photo to find similar products, and the agent can surface options from other stores through Shop Direct ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)). Amazon is rolling out the ability for a request begun by text to carry over to the Amazon Shopping app, Amazon.com and Alexa-enabled devices, and the reverse ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)).

Engadget reports that the agent messages back with suggestions and can add approved items to a cart before completing the order ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2309285/youll-soon-be-able-to-text-alexa-to-shop-on-amazon/)). Amazon states that more than 400 million customers have used Alexa for Shopping in the last 12 months ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop?utm_source=rss)).

Amazon's shopping page states that texting requires a personal Amazon account with a verified mobile number and a phone with RCS chats turned on ([Amazon](http://www.amazon.com/text-alexa-shopping)). It lists most Android phones with Google Messages and iPhones on iOS 18 or later as supporting RCS chats, provided the carrier does too ([Amazon](http://www.amazon.com/text-alexa-shopping)).

TechCrunch reports that the launch follows Amazon's block of Meta's Muse from querying its site in September, and comes just before the start of the holiday shopping season ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/after-blocking-muse-amazon-now-lets-users-text-alexa-to-help-them-shop/)). The outlet adds that DoorDash last month reported third-party agents had placed only hundreds of orders in total on its platform ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/after-blocking-muse-amazon-now-lets-users-text-alexa-to-help-them-shop/)).
