Google announced on 2026-10-19 that Gemini Notebook now offers more creative control from start to finish on Slide Decks and Video Overviews, including a new Review toggle that lets users examine a plan before anything renders ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)). The company said the co-creation capabilities are rolling out globally to all Gemini Notebook users over the upcoming week ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)). Earlier, on 2026-10-16, Google detailed three study features it had recently launched in the product: Short Video Overviews, Interactive Learning Overviews and expanded quiz formats ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/three-tips-study-gemini-notebook/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Google described the co-creation flow as a quick process based directly on the user's sources, replacing a jump from an initial prompt straight to a final output ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)). Users start in the chat and ask Gemini Notebook to create slides or a video overview, and Notebook then suggests topics based on those sources ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)). After the user confirms, the content can be steered further, along with visual style and format ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)).

With the Review toggle on, users can open "Review outline notes" for slides or "Review producer notes" for video in the studio panel ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)). They can add feedback, such as asking for an executive summary at the beginning or swapping the order of a scene, and then select "Proceed" to produce the final artifact ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)). Users who already know what they want can specify it in the prompt and go straight to the review flow ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)). Those who prefer a quick output can skip the toggle and have Notebook generate artifacts as usual ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/)).

In its study post, Google said the Short Video Overview is meant to help users unpack a tricky concept and can be shared with a classmate ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/three-tips-study-gemini-notebook/)). The Interactive Learning Overview weaves together summaries and studio outputs such as slides, quizzes and flashcards, all grounded strictly in the user's coursework, according to Google ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/three-tips-study-gemini-notebook/)). The new quiz formats include short-answer, multi-select and fill-in-the-blank options, and users can ask their notebook for feedback after testing themselves or reviewing flashcards ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/three-tips-study-gemini-notebook/)).
