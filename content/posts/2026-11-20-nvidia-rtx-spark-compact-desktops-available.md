Tom's Hardware reviewed the MSI EdgeMesa N AI+ on 2026-11-20 and listed its price as $7,499.99 in the tested configuration ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)).

## Details (as Reported)

Nvidia describes the desktops as a dedicated Windows PC for local models and always-on agents ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-spark-desktop-local-ai-available-now/)). The company stated that the systems deliver up to 1 petaflop of AI performance and can run 120-billion-parameter large language models locally ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-spark-desktop-local-ai-available-now/)). It also listed Ten-gigabit Ethernet connectivity and said developers can set up agents with Hermes Agent or NVIDIA NemoClaw blueprints and fine-tune supported models using Unsloth ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-spark-desktop-local-ai-available-now/)).

Nvidia added that Switchyard, now available in Hermes, automatically routes agent tasks between local and cloud models ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-spark-desktop-local-ai-available-now/)). The company also announced native Windows on Arm versions of Adobe Illustrator and InDesign, and said Illustrator delivers up to 30% higher performance than its emulated version ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-spark-desktop-local-ai-available-now/)).

Tom's Hardware tested the MSI EdgeMesa N AI+ with 128GB of memory and 4TB of storage ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)). Its verdict credits solid local AI performance for LLMs and image generation, and a familiar Windows 11 experience ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)). The newsroom listed the price as eye-poppingly expensive and the system as less expandable than DGX Spark systems ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)).

On ports, the review found three rear USB Type-C 20 Gbps ports, a 10 Gigabit Ethernet jack and an HDMI 2.1 port ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)). The reviewer noted that the system drops the ConnectX RDMA NIC used to cluster DGX Spark systems ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)). For comparison, it listed MSI's own EdgeXpert with 128GB of memory and a 4TB SSD at $8,099 on sale and $8,499 at full price ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)).

In the AgentPerf-Local benchmark with a Qwen 3.8 27B model and DFlash2 speculative decoding, the EdgeMesa delivered performance practically identical to Microsoft's Surface Laptop Ultra, according to Tom's Hardware ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)). In Geekbench 7 under Windows, AMD's Ryzen AI Halo posted a slightly higher single-threaded score, while its multithreaded score trailed the Nvidia part ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/msi-edgemesa-n-ai-plus-review)).
