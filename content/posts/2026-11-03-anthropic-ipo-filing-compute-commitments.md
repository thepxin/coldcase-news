In an episode published on November 2, 2026, The Register's Kettle podcast examined the 614-page initial public offering filing that Anthropic has made public ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)). Systems editor Tobias Mann told host Brandon Vigliarolo that Anthropic's operating losses were about 700 million dollars ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)). The newsroom's summary put the company's compute commitments at nearly a trillion dollars ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)).

## Details (as Reported)

Mann described the filing as a mix of risk mitigation that reads like a doomsday prophecy, as most S-1 filings do ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)). He noted that, for the first time in the company's history, revenues outpaced compute expenses two to one ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)). Vigliarolo observed that day-to-day business losses of 693 million dollars were down from 1.2 billion dollars in an earlier comparison period ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)).

On the 80.7 billion dollars in total losses, Mann believes a large part is the write-off of equity the company will have to pay off when it goes public ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)). He explained that Anthropic's business is cash heavy and that partners provided capital for compute either as debt or as repayment in equity ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)).

The newsroom's summary also states that much of the company's computing power relies on customers who are also competitors ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)). Senior reporter Tom Claburn pointed to inference costs, noting that inference has become cheaper per token over the past year ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- Mann said the filing does not specifically explain the 80.7 billion dollars in total losses ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)).
- Claburn asked whether the company is aiming for a two trillion dollar valuation, and Mann replied that the number depends on whether the company can sustain its results ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)).
- Claburn said the company has not demonstrated that the use case for AI is as wide at volume as it claims ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/02/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-is-big-on-promises-short-on-answers-to-tough-questions/5306811)).
