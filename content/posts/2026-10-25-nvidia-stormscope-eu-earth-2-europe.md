NVIDIA announced on 2026-10-22 that StormScope EU, a regional version of its StormScope model for short-term forecasting, has joined the Earth-2 family of open AI models ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)). The model was trained on European weather and climate data and uses EUMETSAT satellite observations to predict evolving cloud conditions across 16 Meteosat channels at 10-minute intervals ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)). At GTC Berlin, the company is showcasing a live StormScope EU pipeline in an interactive weather demo built in partnership with Earthmover ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).

## Details (as Reported)


- Earth-2 gives developers and researchers a family of open AI models and tools they can customize with their own data, running on NVIDIA computing ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- The demo uses Earth2Studio to run forecasts and Earthmover's data platform to manage and serve the real-time results, which update every 10 minutes as new satellite observations come in ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- Earthmover serves the results as map tiles, which lets visitors explore predictions across Europe without downloading entire datasets ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- Developers can combine StormScope for short-term forecasting with CorrDiff to add local detail to coarser weather data ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- The open source PhysicsNeMo framework provides training and customization tools for applications that incorporate proprietary data and domain expertise ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- AXA is using FourCastNet 3 to study regional cyclone risk beyond the historical record across more than 1,000 simulated seasons ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- Vaisala Xweather is combining Earth-2 with its proprietary observations and lightning data, and is adapting StormScope with lightning data to advance short-term forecasting lead time ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- University of Manchester researchers adapted CorrDiff for U.K. air-quality modeling, trained it on the Isambard-AI supercomputer and ran it locally on NVIDIA DGX Spark systems ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- TotalEnergies is exploring Earth-2 for solar-energy forecasting and is evaluating StormScope-based solar-radiation nowcasts at two photovoltaic sites ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
- Space42 developed SARDiff4Height using CorrDiff and PhysicsNeMo, a project that estimates surface heights from a single radar image and produces uncertainty maps ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/europe-earth-2-open-models-weather-climate-prediction/)).
