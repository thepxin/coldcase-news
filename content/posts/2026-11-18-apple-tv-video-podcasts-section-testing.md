Apple began testing a video podcasts section in Apple TV on Monday, with a full rollout to all users promised "soon" ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)). The company shared the news with The New York Times, according to 9to5Mac ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)). The section will also appear in the Apple TV app on other devices such as smart TVs ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)).

## Details (as Reported)

According to 9to5Mac, the section will be free to all users soon, and Apple also plans to increase the number of video podcasts on its regular Podcasts app ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)). Oliver Schusser, an Apple vice president who oversees services including podcasts, television and music, said Apple wants podcasts available wherever people want to enjoy them, on small screens and big screens ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)). He described the move as being about discovery and helping creators reach a new audience, and added: "We didn’t approach this in a way that would financially benefit us" ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)).

Podcasts began as audio only, but it has become increasingly common for them to be released as video too, which extends their reach to platforms such as YouTube, Hulu and Netflix ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)). Creators have welcomed the move, one noting that people who see a clip on Instagram, TikTok or Twitter can now open the Apple app and watch the video ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)). Ms. Martin of NPR remarked that "Everything is TV now, which is a little bit heartbreaking for a lifelong audio person to say" ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/16/video-podcasts-coming-soon-to-apple-tv-and-other-devices/)).

In its own announcement a day later, Apple said Baby, this is Keke Palmer consistently ranks among the top video and top comedy shows on Apple Podcasts ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-podcasts-names-baby-this-is-keke-palmer-the-2026-show-of-the-year/)). Schusser added that video brings that connection even closer ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-podcasts-names-baby-this-is-keke-palmer-the-2026-show-of-the-year/)).
