Apple reported fiscal 2026 fourth quarter results on November 2, 2026, with quarterly revenue of $114.5 billion, up 12 percent year over year ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-reports-fourth-quarter-results/)). The company described records for total revenue, iPhone revenue and EPS for a September quarter ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-reports-fourth-quarter-results/)). The iPhone Duo contributed no sales to the period, because it went on sale in October ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/02/john-ternus-addresses-iphone-duo-supply-constraints-display-issue/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Apple stated that company gross margin was 47.9 percent, including a favorable impact of approximately 1 percentage point from tariff refunds ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-reports-fourth-quarter-results/)). Fiscal 2026 revenue reached an all-time record of $479 billion, according to CFO Kevan Parekh, who also reported $28 billion in operating cash flow and $28 billion returned to shareholders in the quarter ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-reports-fourth-quarter-results/)). Services revenue reached a new all-time high ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-reports-fourth-quarter-results/)).

The Verge reported that Apple posted record iPhone revenue even without a standard iPhone 18 model ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1024915/apple-iphone-duo-supply-chain-constraints)). CEO John Ternus named the iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max in Apple's release ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-reports-fourth-quarter-results/)).

In an interview with Reuters, Ternus stated that the Duo is constrained as of today and that Apple is not able to predict when supply and demand will be in balance ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/02/john-ternus-addresses-iphone-duo-supply-constraints-display-issue/)). On the earnings call, Chief Operating Officer Sabih Khan gave a similar assessment ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/apple-says-its-hard-to-predict-when-iphone-duo-constraints-will-improve/)). Apple Store shipping estimates have stayed at late January since the first preorders sold out on October 16 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/02/john-ternus-addresses-iphone-duo-supply-constraints-display-issue/)). The Verge reported that the foldable launched for $1,999 with a 5.4 outer display and a 7.6-inch inner screen ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1024915/apple-iphone-duo-supply-chain-constraints)).

Ternus told Reuters that a very small percentage of Duo units were returned with display issues and that Apple is replacing them ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/02/john-ternus-addresses-iphone-duo-supply-constraints-display-issue/)). TechCrunch reported that Verizon has offered gift cards to customers over the delays and that Apple is deepening its partnership with the titanium producer Perryman ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/apple-says-its-hard-to-predict-when-iphone-duo-constraints-will-improve/)).

On memory costs, Ternus described current pricing as unprecedented during the call and expects even higher memory costs in the December quarter, partly offset by recent price increases ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1024915/apple-iphone-duo-supply-chain-constraints)). Khan noted that Apple raised prices on a number of products reluctantly ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/apple-says-its-hard-to-predict-when-iphone-duo-constraints-will-improve/)).
