Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 shows electric vehicles as ordinary traffic in Vice City, according to a report published by Electrek on 2026-11-21, and Electrify America has responded to the game's parody of its charging network ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)). The game was released on 2026-11-19 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/19/calling-in-sick-vice-city/)). The Volkswagen-owned network stated that it "will stick to charging vehicles in the real world" and added, "We wish ReVolt America well" ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Electrek's reporter wrote that the game begins in the Keys, where older cars dominate and few EVs appear, and that EVs and charging stations multiply on the way north to Vice City ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)). The reporter also found a charging hub at the power plant with dozens of stalls, and chargers blocked by gas cars parked in EV spots ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)).

In Electrek's testing, the in-game EVs have about the same range as the gas cars, charge as fast as a fill-up and cost the same or less to top up ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)). Kotaku's highway-loop test, as cited by Electrek, put the Banshee sports car at 18 minutes and 31 seconds and an old pickup at 29 minutes and 8 seconds ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)). Kotaku reported that crossing the whole map by highway takes under 15 minutes ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)).

Florida had a 9.4% share of new passenger vehicle sales in Q2 2026 against 7.4% nationally, according to the Southern Alliance for Clean Energy ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)). Real Florida has roughly three gas stations for every public charging location, based on counts of 3,267 charging locations from the Alliance for Automotive Innovation and more than 10,500 gas stations from xMap ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/gta-6-electric-vehicles-mainstream-vice-city/)).
