Nintendo has taken $50 off its Nintendo Switch 2 Mario Kart World bundle, with the reduced price running from November 22 through Saturday, December 5 ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1038181/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deal-mario-kart-world-bundle-2026-sale)). The bundle is priced at $499.99, previously $549.99, and includes the console, a digital copy of Mario Kart World and three months of Nintendo Switch Online ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1038181/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deal-mario-kart-world-bundle-2026-sale)). The cut temporarily rolls back the $50 price increase that went into effect in early September ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1038181/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deal-mario-kart-world-bundle-2026-sale)).

## Details (as Reported)

Tom's Hardware reports that Nintendo raised the Switch 2 price earlier this year in response to rising NAND and DRAM prices ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/handheld-gaming/nintendo-switch-2-bundles-drop-to-pre-hike-pricing-in-black-friday-run-up-sale-get-a-switch-2-along-with-mario-kart-world-and-nintendo-switch-online-for-usd50-off)). It describes the Mario Kart World bundle as normally selling for $550, with Amazon marking it down by $50 to its original price of $499 ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/handheld-gaming/nintendo-switch-2-bundles-drop-to-pre-hike-pricing-in-black-friday-run-up-sale-get-a-switch-2-along-with-mario-kart-world-and-nintendo-switch-online-for-usd50-off)). Amazon extends the same discount to bundles that add a Switch 2 Pro Controller, the Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake or a microSD Express card ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/handheld-gaming/nintendo-switch-2-bundles-drop-to-pre-hike-pricing-in-black-friday-run-up-sale-get-a-switch-2-along-with-mario-kart-world-and-nintendo-switch-online-for-usd50-off)). In the base bundle, the game is a redeemable code rather than a physical cartridge ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/handheld-gaming/nintendo-switch-2-bundles-drop-to-pre-hike-pricing-in-black-friday-run-up-sale-get-a-switch-2-along-with-mario-kart-world-and-nintendo-switch-online-for-usd50-off)).

The Verge lists a second current console bundle, the Nintendo Switch Sports Resort bundle at $529.99, and states its price is unchanged for Black Friday ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1038181/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deal-mario-kart-world-bundle-2026-sale)). Wired prices that bundle at $530 and describes it as around $28 off the usual price ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/best-black-friday-nintendo-switch-deals-11-22-26/)). Wired puts the Mario Kart World bundle at $500, a $50 saving ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/best-black-friday-nintendo-switch-deals-11-22-26/)).

Separately, Nintendo is taking $20 off several Switch 2 games, including Donkey Kong Bananza at $49.99 from $69.99 ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1037313/nintendo-switch-2-game-black-friday-cyber-monday-deal-sale)). Wired also lists Metroid Prime 4: Beyond, Kirby Air Riders and Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 at $50 each ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/best-black-friday-nintendo-switch-deals-11-22-26/)).
