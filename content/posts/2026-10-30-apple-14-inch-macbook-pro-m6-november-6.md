Apple announced the 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 on October 27, 2026, and set Friday, November 6 as the date availability starts ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). The laptop is offered in space black and silver, with pre-orders beginning October 28 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). The company describes it as a laptop for college students, business users and creators ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The M6 chip has a 12-core CPU with two more cores than before, and a 12-core GPU that also has two additional cores ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). Apple states the GPU delivers up to 30 percent faster graphics and up to 25 percent faster AI workloads than M5 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). The Dual 16-core Neural Engine delivers up to 1.9x faster performance than M5, according to the company ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)).

Memory bandwidth rises to up to 170GB/s ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). Apple lists Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 for connectivity, and macOS 27 as the operating system pairing ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). The Liquid Retina XDR display delivers up to 1600 nits of peak HDR brightness and a 1,000,000:1 contrast ratio, with a nano-texture option ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). The laptop also has a 12MP Center Stage camera and a six-speaker sound system ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)).

Apple compares the laptop with the 13-inch MacBook Pro with M1 as well, citing up to 13x faster time to first token for LLM prompt processing and up to 11.7x faster ray-traced rendering in Blender ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)). Against the 14-inch MacBook Pro with M5, the company cites up to 1.3x faster Xcode project builds ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)).

Shelly Goldberg, Apple's vice president of Hardware Engineering, said the M6 chip takes performance to the next level with a boost in CPU speed and graphics and more powerful on-device AI capabilities ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/)).
