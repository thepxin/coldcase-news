Blue Origin has pushed the next New Glenn launch to January 2027, according to an internal memo from CEO Dave Limp that the Financial Times first reported ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)). A New Glenn rocket exploded on its launchpad in Florida during a hot-test in May 2026, causing notable damage to the company's infrastructure ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)). Shortly afterward, the CEO claimed the repairs would be finished quickly and that the next launch would occur before the end of the year ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The memo states that the launchpad's Vehicle Access Tower has been repaired and restacked and that the launch bridge has been restored ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)). Limp credited weather issues for the fixes happening more slowly than hoped ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)). He also wrote: "Your collective work on our return to flight has been nothing short of remarkable," and "In just six months, Team Blue has accomplished what many thought would take years" ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)).

Ars Technica reported that Blue Origin packed a lot of news into one Thursday afternoon, with the reveal of a new kick stage for New Glenn and a new rocket engine design alongside the delay ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/rocket-report-new-glenn-wont-fly-this-year-china-launches-mini-falcon-heavy/)). Reconstruction of the wrecked launch pad at Cape Canaveral, Florida, continues after a catastrophic explosion nearly six months ago ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/rocket-report-new-glenn-wont-fly-this-year-china-launches-mini-falcon-heavy/)). The outlet reported pent-up demand from NASA, Amazon and the Space Force for New Glenn to return to action ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/rocket-report-new-glenn-wont-fly-this-year-china-launches-mini-falcon-heavy/)).

Blue Origin won a NASA contract in September to set up a telecommunications network for Mars ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)). New Glenn rockets are also slated to carry more equipment into space for Amazon's Leo satellite broadband service ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308647/blue-origin-new-glenn-launch-delay-will-continue-into-2027/)). Analytics firm Quilty Space listed developmental delays in new rockets, including New Glenn, among the factors behind a shortage of launches outside China ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/rocket-report-new-glenn-wont-fly-this-year-china-launches-mini-falcon-heavy/)).
