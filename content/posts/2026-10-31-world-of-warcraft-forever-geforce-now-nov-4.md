## Details (as Reported)

NVIDIA described World of Warcraft: Forever as an iconic world reimagined with new and expanded zones, new gameplay enhancements and upgraded graphics ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)). PT ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)). It is aimed at new WoW users who do not already have a subscription, while supplies last, on a first-come, first-served basis ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)). Once it is available, members can sign in to their GeForce NOW account and redeem it in the Account Portal ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)).

The week's lineup also includes nine games joining the GeForce NOW library ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)). Mod.io support is now available directly in game for Cyberpunk 2077 and The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Remastered ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)).

For Phantom Blade Zero, NVIDIA stated that Ultimate members can wield GeForce RTX 5080-class performance in the cloud across PCs, Macs, handhelds, mobile devices, browsers and compatible TVs ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)). The game is powered by Unreal Engine 5 and combines wuxia storytelling with cinematic action ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-phantom-blade-zero/)). PlayStation's blog describes it as an action-RPG that draws on Soulslikes, hack-and-slash combat, RPGs and search action games ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/phantom-blade-zero-how-boss-weapons-transform-combat-and-exploration/)).
