GG Technologies, a startup headquartered in Santa Monica, filed a lawsuit against Apple in the Bombay High Court in October, alleging that the iPhone maker's NameDrop feature infringes its Indian patent and seeking ₹15 billion (about $161.86 million) in damages ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).

The company's 781-page filing also asks the court to restrict Apple from selling or importing iPhones and Apple Watches preloaded with NameDrop in India ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)). The suit followed mediation proceedings that began in April and failed in September, as TechCrunch reported ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).

Apple announced iOS 17 on June 5, 2023, and presented NameDrop as a feature that allows users to share contact information by bringing their iPhones together ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/06/ios-17-makes-iphone-more-personal-and-intuitive/)). The company did not respond to a request for comment from TechCrunch ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).

GG Technologies also sued Apple in the United States in April over related patent infringement claims ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)). The United States International Trade Commission opened an investigation into the alleged infringement in August, following a complaint from the startup, according to TechCrunch ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).

## Details (as Reported)

- TechCrunch reported that Gaurav Goel, a graduate of the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi and a former Goldman Sachs investment banker, founded GG Technologies in 2018 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).
- According to TechCrunch, GG Technologies developed StayTouch as an alternative to exchanging business cards, and the app, which is available on iOS and Android, lets users exchange contact details by bringing their phones close together ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).
- In its court filing, GG Technologies alleged that NameDrop infringes its Indian patent ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).
- Before the trade commission, the startup is seeking an order that could block imports of infringing Apple devices into the United States ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).
- TechCrunch reported that iPhones still account for less than 10 per cent of smartphone shipments in India ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/startup-sues-apple-in-india-over-tap-to-share-contacts-feature/)).
