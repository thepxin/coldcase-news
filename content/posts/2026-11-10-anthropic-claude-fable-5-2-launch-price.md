Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, describing them as models built for the most daunting technical challenges ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). Fable 5.2 is available at launch, while Mythos 5.2 begins with vetted organizations ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030005/anthropic-claude-fable-mythos-5-2)). The company priced Fable at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)).

## Timeline

- **2026-11-09**: Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)).

## Details (as Reported)

Anthropic stated that Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 show improvements in coding, reasoning, and vision, and that testers found them clearer in communication than past models ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). Its benchmark table lists Fable 5.2 at 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 at Xhigh effort, against 32.4% for Fable 5.1 and 45.5% for Opus 5.5 ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). The same table lists 69.9% on FrontierSWE v2 for Fable 5.2, against 65.5% for GPT-6 Astra ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)).

TechCrunch reported that Fable's input price fell from $10 to $8 per million tokens, which makes Fable 5.2 cheaper than GPT-6 Astra ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-releases-new-mythos-and-fable-models-amid-rsi-concerns/)). It also reported that Fable 5.2 is compatible with zero data retention policies through the Enterprise Frontier Safeguards system ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-releases-new-mythos-and-fable-models-amid-rsi-concerns/)).

Anthropic recommends that most users start with Claude Opus 5.5 for coding and knowledge tasks, moving up to Fable or Mythos for the most ambitious technical work ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). It added that Fable 5.2 can act as an Advisor for smaller models or as an Orchestrator that delegates simpler tasks ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)).

For safety, Anthropic stated that Fable 5.2's cyber and biology safeguards are as robust as those of Fable 5.1 but produce fewer unnecessary blocks ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). The company also stated that it has implemented its strongest-ever protections against distillation attacks ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)).

Subscribers on eligible paid plans receive extra Fable usage this week, with one to three Fable resets depending on how long they have subscribed ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-fable-5-2-model-details-here/)).
