A new trailer accompanied the announcement, along with a closer look at combat, companions and exploration ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)).

## Details (as Reported)

In the game, the first mission for the Heroes Guild takes an unexpected turn when a mysterious maelstrom leaves the player stranded on an unfamiliar island ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). Beyond its shores lie dangerous creatures, suspicious alchemists and a bustling town with people to meet and side quests to discover ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)).

Players create a hero by choosing appearance, background, talents and skills ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). The progression system lets players combine skills across disciplines freely, as opposed to a more rigid class system ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). Companions each bring a distinct history and personal quests, and their equipment, abilities and attributes can be completely customized ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). Friendships and romance can become part of the journey ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)).

Combat is tactical and turn-based, with approximately 200 skills across ten disciplines ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). Surface interactions and weather matter in battle, and they can alter how skills work ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). In one example from the announcement, a Geomancer summons a mandrake, a Hydromancer makes it rain so the mandrake grows, and an Aeromancer uses lightning to electrify the puddles and paralyze anyone standing in them ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)).

Looting, mining and gathering herbs provide materials that can be crafted into weapons, equipment, arrows, potions and food ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). Side quests include solving a murder mystery, hunting feared monsters for bounty money and settling a deadly dispute between farm animals ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)). Hidden treasures, puzzles, ancient books and forgotten notes reward exploration of the Respite Archipelago ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/guild-saga-vanished-worlds-brings-tactical-adventure-to-ps5-in-may-2027/)).
