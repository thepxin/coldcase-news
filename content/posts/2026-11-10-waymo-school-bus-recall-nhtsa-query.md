The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) opened a recall query into Waymo in a filing dated November 6, seeking to assess whether the company's software remedy was adequate for robotaxis that passed stopped school buses ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/)). The regulator noted that a majority of the reported incidents occurred after the remedy date ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2310872/nhtsa-investigates-if-waymo-robotaxis-are-still-running-school-bus-stop-signs/)). Waymo said it has received no citations since March 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The Office of Defects Investigation opened its initial investigation into Waymo in October 2025 after footage showed a Waymo vehicle maneuvering around a stopped school bus in Atlanta ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/)). The bus had its stop sign extended and its lights flashing, and it was unloading children ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/)). Austin Independent School District officials documented further incidents in Austin, reporting at least five of them in a letter filed with NHTSA ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/)).

According to a filing, Waymo issued a fix in mid-November and an official voluntary recall a month later ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/waymo-school-bus-problem-the-us-government-says-it-didnt-work/)). A report from the National Transportation Safety Board found that another school bus passing incident occurred in mid-January, several weeks after the supposed fix ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/waymo-school-bus-problem-the-us-government-says-it-didnt-work/)). Wired also reported that the Austin school district helped set up a half-day data collection event with school buses and stop-arm signals after Waymo had issued its fix and recall, and that incidents continued ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/waymo-school-bus-problem-the-us-government-says-it-didnt-work/)).

In a statement, Waymo spokesperson Dana Gallagher stated that the company has made significant improvements to vehicle behavior around school buses ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/waymo-school-bus-problem-the-us-government-says-it-didnt-work/)). The company added that it looks forward to continuing its engagement on recall processes for AV software that continues to learn and improve ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2310872/nhtsa-investigates-if-waymo-robotaxis-are-still-running-school-bus-stop-signs/)).

The Office of Defects Investigation also opened a probe into an incident in which a Waymo robotaxi hit a child in Santa Monica, California, in January ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2310872/nhtsa-investigates-if-waymo-robotaxis-are-still-running-school-bus-stop-signs/)). The NTSB, an independent federal agency, launched its own investigation into Waymo's behavior around stopped school buses earlier this year ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2310872/nhtsa-investigates-if-waymo-robotaxis-are-still-running-school-bus-stop-signs/)). Wired reported that in years past, recall queries like this one have led to civil penalties, some costing automakers and software developers tens of millions of dollars ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/waymo-school-bus-problem-the-us-government-says-it-didnt-work/)).

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- Whether the software update fixed the problem is what the recall query will determine ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2310872/nhtsa-investigates-if-waymo-robotaxis-are-still-running-school-bus-stop-signs/)).
- The causes of the conditions behind the post-remedy reports remain for NHTSA to identify ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2310872/nhtsa-investigates-if-waymo-robotaxis-are-still-running-school-bus-stop-signs/)).
- Waymo's response to the query is pending, as the company stated it will respond once the query is received ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/)).
- Austin Independent School District officials had not yet responded to TechCrunch's request for comment ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/waymo-robotaxis-kept-illegally-passing-school-buses-after-software-fix-feds-say/)).
