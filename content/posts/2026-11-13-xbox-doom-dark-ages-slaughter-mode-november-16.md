Xbox announced at the close of its eight-city FanFest tour in Seattle that Slaughter Mode arrives in DOOM: The Dark Ages on November 16 ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)). The mode is available at no additional cost to anyone who owns the Revelations DLC, the Premium Edition, or the Premium Upgrade ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)). The announcement was part of a recap marking 25 years of XBOX, which also listed several in-game items with their own dates ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Xbox described Slaughter Mode as escalating rounds of randomized enemies, bosses and arenas, with rewards that stack and grow the more a player plays ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)). Once the Gears of War: E-Day shop rotates on November 17, the weapon skin set can be acquired with the free-to-earn currency called Coins or bought with premium currency ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)).

The Minecraft item requires Minecraft: Bedrock Edition, which is sold separately, and allows one item per person or account, redeemable by December 10, 2026 at 10 AM PT ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)). The recap also listed a Forza Horizon 6 livery, a Halo: Campaign Evolved armor style, a Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 livery set and a Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 blueprint for digital pre-orders on XBOX Series X|S ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)).

In Candy Crush Saga, players earn pins as they progress through levels, with characters and moments from Halo, Fallout, Sea of Thieves, Forza, DOOM, Banjo-Kazooie, Crash Bandicoot and Spyro ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/candy-crush-saga-game-on-halo-fallout-sea-of-thieves-xbox-25/)). The season is available on iOS and Android, and VIP players in the US can enter a contest at candycrush.com for one of 1,000 limited-edition physical pin packs ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/candy-crush-saga-game-on-halo-fallout-sea-of-thieves-xbox-25/)).

Xbox also announced a free Winter Update bringing Firefight to Halo: Campaign Evolved and said 25 original XBOX games are now on PC through XBOX Backward Compatibility ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)).
