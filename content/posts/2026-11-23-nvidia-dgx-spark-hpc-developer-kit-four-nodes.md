NVIDIA introduced the DGX Spark HPC Developer Kit on November 16, 2026, at SC26, the annual supercomputing conference held that week in Chicago ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). The kit is a four-node cluster of NVIDIA DGX Spark systems, each linked directly to its two neighbors in a ring ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). The company aims it at researchers, academics and developers who would otherwise wait for time on a shared GPU cluster ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The kit scales from one system to four over NVIDIA ConnectX-7 networking, NVIDIA reports ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). With NVFP4, researchers can deploy models of up to 700B parameters locally across the four systems, such as DeepSeek V4.1 ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). Teams can also develop and debug on their own, saving server hours for production runs and keeping unpublished results and proprietary datasets on premises ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)).

Among demonstrated four-system workloads, NVIDIA listed LAMMPS with Kokkos for materials simulation and NAMD for molecular dynamics ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). The broader DGX Spark community has validated GROMACS, OpenFOAM, WRF and NVIDIA NIM microservices such as OpenFold3 and Boltz-2, the company adds ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)).

Kvantify reports running tensor-network simulations of quantum-chemistry circuits with up to 70 qubits and 975 gates on a single DGX Spark ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). The Rubin Lab at IGI at the University of California, Berkeley uses a DGX Spark and the NVIDIA Evo 2 model ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). With no 11-hour cluster queue times, results arrived 15x sooner, NVIDIA notes ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). On a single device, the Hartwig Group at UC Berkeley fit a 120B-class model using NVFP4 quantization for LoRA fine-tuning ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)).

Buyers can purchase DGX Spark products and cables from OEM partners and through NVIDIA Marketplace ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)). Acer, ASUS, Dell Technologies, GIGABYTE, H3C, HP, Lenovo, MSI and xFusion are the listed partners ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/dgx-spark-hpc-developer-kit-sc26/)).
