Apple and F1 Academy announced on November 19, 2026 that the all-female racing series will stream on Apple TV in the United States beginning in 2027 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)). Fans will be able to watch every qualifying session and race throughout the season ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)). The 2027 season begins on March 19 at the Jeddah Corniche Circuit in Saudi Arabia, Engadget reports ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2322221/apple-tv-expands-its-racing-coverage-with-the-all-female-f1-academy/)).

The announcement follows a five-year broadcast partnership that made Apple TV the exclusive U.S. broadcast partner for Formula 1 starting in 2026 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)). The Formula 1 deal is set to run through 2031, 9to5Mac reports ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/apple-tv-f1-academy-stream-next-year/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The series enters its fifth season in 2027, and it is developing the next generation of female racing talent ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)). Apple describes F1 Academy as the second-most-followed series after Formula 1, citing the 2025 Global F1 Fan Survey ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)). Engadget echoes that ranking, presenting it as Apple's claim ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2322221/apple-tv-expands-its-racing-coverage-with-the-all-female-f1-academy/)).

Viewers need an Apple TV subscription, which does not cover third-party branded subscription services or content available for rental or purchase in the Apple TV app ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)). The company bills the viewing experience as premium, with customizable features ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)).

Susie Wolff, F1 Academy's managing director, called the agreement a landmark partnership and a step in the growth of the series ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)). Apple's announcement names drivers including Alba Hurup Larsen, Alisha Palmowski and Emma Felbermayr among the series' roster of talented young drivers ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/)).

Apple and Engadget place F1 Academy's launch in 2022, while 9to5Mac dates it to 2023 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2322221/apple-tv-expands-its-racing-coverage-with-the-all-female-f1-academy/); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/apple-tv-f1-academy-stream-next-year/)). The schedule, according to 9to5Mac, runs across seven races through November ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/apple-tv-f1-academy-stream-next-year/)).
