9to5Mac published a hands-on review of the Kuxiu K4 Air on November 15, 2026, describing a 3-in-1 MagSafe charger that is 8.5mm thin when folded and can charge an iPhone wirelessly at up to 25W ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/hands-on-this-25w-magsafe-charger-is-thinner-than-an-iphone-kuxiu-k4-air-video/)). The reviewer reports it is back in stock after a limited initial run and costs $79 on Kuxiu's website ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/hands-on-this-25w-magsafe-charger-is-thinner-than-an-iphone-kuxiu-k4-air-video/)). Kuxiu's own product page lists the folded size as 102.7 × 64 × 8.5 mm and the weight as 120 g ([Kuxiu](https://kuxiu.com/collections/new-arrivals/products/kuxiu-k4-air-burgundy)).

## Details (as Reported)

The reviewer spent a few weeks with the charger and traveled across the Atlantic with it ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/hands-on-this-25w-magsafe-charger-is-thinner-than-an-iphone-kuxiu-k4-air-video/)). For reference, the reviewer's iPhone 17 Pro measures roughly 8.25mm thick, while the K4 Air comes in at 8.5mm ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/hands-on-this-25w-magsafe-charger-is-thinner-than-an-iphone-kuxiu-k4-air-video/)). The review describes CNC-machined aluminum, a magnetic main surface for the iPhone, a second surface for AirPods with added magnets, and a dedicated Apple Watch charger that fits an Apple Watch Ultra 4 ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/hands-on-this-25w-magsafe-charger-is-thinner-than-an-iphone-kuxiu-k4-air-video/)).

With an iPhone Duo opened in landscape mode, the reviewer reports the phone tilts slightly to the left, while portrait mode works well and a folded iPhone Duo charges on a nightstand without issues ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/hands-on-this-25w-magsafe-charger-is-thinner-than-an-iphone-kuxiu-k4-air-video/)). The reviewer adds that the form factor can technically charge two iPhones at once ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/hands-on-this-25w-magsafe-charger-is-thinner-than-an-iphone-kuxiu-k4-air-video/)).

Kuxiu states that the stand uses 32 N52 magnets, is UL, FCC and CE certified, and includes over-current, over-voltage and over-temperature protection with foreign object detection ([Kuxiu](https://kuxiu.com/collections/new-arrivals/products/kuxiu-k4-air-burgundy)). The company offers a 2-year warranty and a 30-day money-back guarantee, and says orders ship within 1 business day ([Kuxiu](https://kuxiu.com/collections/new-arrivals/products/kuxiu-k4-air-burgundy)). Its Black Friday offer uses the code BLACK26, and buying 3 or more items unlocks 20% off automatically ([Kuxiu](https://kuxiu.com/collections/new-arrivals/products/kuxiu-k4-air-burgundy)).
