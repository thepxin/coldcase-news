AAWireless, the original wireless Android Auto adapter brand, introduced its compact Nexus and Nano adapters this week, with an actual release date of December 8 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)). Pre-registration opened on November 18, and early orders are available starting on December 1 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)). Both adapters are priced at $69.99, or $74.99 on Amazon ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The Nexus and Nano are both smaller than AAWireless's main Two and Two+ models ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)). The Nexus has a circular puck design with a USB-C plug and a replaceable cable ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)). The Nano is an adapter on the end of a USB-A plug ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)).

Both adapters have an upgraded chipset, Wi-Fi 6 support, and support for both Android Auto and CarPlay ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)). Like the AAWireless Two series, they also have physical buttons ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/aawireless-nexus-nano-adapters-release/)).

The Motorola MA2, designed with Google, was announced in March 2026 as the sequel to the MA1, which launched in 2022 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/13/motorola-ma2-android-auto-amazon-delay/)). Its Amazon listing is still live but sold out, and it shows a delivery date in mid-late December ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/13/motorola-ma2-android-auto-amazon-delay/)).

Twelve South launched the Airfly Drive at $39.99, and the company states it works with Android Auto as well as CarPlay ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/twelve-south-launches-tiny-new-airfly-drive-wireless-carplay-adapter/)). It has a USB-A connector, and a USB-C adapter is included in the box ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/twelve-south-launches-tiny-new-airfly-drive-wireless-carplay-adapter/)).
