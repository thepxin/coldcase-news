Levent Alpöge, a mathematician employed by Anthropic, published a paper this week claiming a proof of the Riemann hypothesis found with the company's Claude model ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-have-solved-167-year-old-math-problem-with-claude-riemann-hypothesis-proof-allegedly-discovered-but-remains-unconfirmed)). He wrote: "I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis" ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-have-solved-167-year-old-math-problem-with-claude-riemann-hypothesis-proof-allegedly-discovered-but-remains-unconfirmed)). The hypothesis dates to 1859 and is among the Millennium Prize problems ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)).

According to TechCrunch, several participants in a Zulip chat of mathematicians, including MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland, confirmed that Alpöge's solution compiles in Lean, a coding language used to formally verify proofs ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)). Tom's Hardware reports that the result remains unconfirmed ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-have-solved-167-year-old-math-problem-with-claude-riemann-hypothesis-proof-allegedly-discovered-but-remains-unconfirmed)).

## Details (as Reported)

Alpöge described a Lean-compilable solution and wrote that he messaged Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei last Sunday night to ask how to announce it ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)). He also believes the method could extend to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis and the Extended Riemann Hypothesis ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-have-solved-167-year-old-math-problem-with-claude-riemann-hypothesis-proof-allegedly-discovered-but-remains-unconfirmed)).

In the Zulip chat, Chris Birkbeck of the University of East Anglia wrote that the cohomology bound is proved by what looks like an automorphy-lifting argument ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)). TechCrunch reports that most participants appear confident the formalized Lean result will hold ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)).

Vakil, a Stanford professor and president of the American Mathematics Association, wrote that Masullo's draft is "all human, with no AI" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)). Masullo described the work as the result of 12 years ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)).
