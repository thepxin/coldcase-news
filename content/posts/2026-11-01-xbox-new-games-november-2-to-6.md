Xbox announced on October 30 its lineup of games coming to Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Xbox on PC and Game Pass for November 2 to 6 ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/30/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-2-to-6/)). The slate opens with GRAVHOUNDS in Game Preview on November 2 and includes Void/Breaker 1.0 on November 4 and Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School on November 5 ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/30/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-2-to-6/)). The company added that release dates are subject to change ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/30/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-2-to-6/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Xbox describes GRAVHOUNDS as a 1 to 4 player co-op action builder in which players build and defend a base and extract before the danger grows ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/30/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-2-to-6/)). ZERO PARADES is described as an espionage RPG from the creators of Disco Elysium ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/30/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-2-to-6/)).

For Football Manager 27, pre-orders on PC, Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass grant early access starting on November 3 ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/30/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-2-to-6/)). Xbox describes Escape Academy 2 as a puzzle game with online or local co-op ([Xbox (Microsoft)](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/30/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-2-to-6/)).

On the PlayStation side, the podcast also lists Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered and Let's Sing 2027 for PS5 on November 3, and Where The Seeds Fall on November 5 ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/30/official-playstation-podcast-episode-552-shivering-silver/)). Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 begins on November 6 ([PlayStation (Sony)](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/30/official-playstation-podcast-episode-552-shivering-silver/)).
