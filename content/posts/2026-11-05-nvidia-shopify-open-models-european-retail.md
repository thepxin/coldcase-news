On 2026-10-22, NVIDIA published an account of European retailers using its open models and physical AI tools, led by a commerce platform that it says cut inference costs by 90% ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). Shopify post-trained NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning for the GraphQL subagent in Sidekick, its AI assistant for merchants ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). On Shopify's internal benchmark, the post-trained model surpasses a leading frontier model in accuracy while cutting serving costs by 90% and delivering responses 7x faster, according to NVIDIA ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Shopify's engineers use the NVIDIA Megatron Bridge library for faster fine-tuning and NVIDIA NeMo RL for reinforcement learning ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). NVIDIA reported that its CCluster vLLM inference optimization software helped Shopify reach 4.5x faster performance and up to 12x lower inference cost across five additional use cases ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). Those use cases are catalog enrichment, merchant assistant, e-commerce shopping simulation, product taxonomy classification, and merchant and buyer recommendations ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)).

Zooplus worked with Infosys and NVIDIA technologies to rebuild its product discovery stack, using the NVIDIA Nemotron Retriever library for semantic search ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). Photoroom's Image Editing API now standardizes every primary image for Vestiaire Collective at the point of listing, and listings it processed sold at a 5% higher rate of liquidity, with 3% to 7% more unique views per product page ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)).

Omi launched ProductDrop, which anchors every generated image to an approved digital twin, and L'Oréal is using the approach within its CreAItech program ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). L'Oréal is also integrating NVIDIA ALCHEMI into its research, moving from months of experimentation to hours of computational screening ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). Orbital Industries' OrbMol-v2 is five times faster than comparable models and delivers 21% lower error than the next-best model of its kind, per NVIDIA ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)).

Otto Group advanced its autonomous warehouse under human supervision initiative to its largest fulfillment center in Haldensleben, Germany, built with Reply on NVIDIA Omniverse libraries and running on Google Cloud ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)). Logistics teams can design, test and optimize warehouse operations entirely in simulation before any physical implementation ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/european-retail-open-models-robotics/)).
