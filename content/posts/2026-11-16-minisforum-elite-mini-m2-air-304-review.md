Tom's Hardware reviewed the Minisforum Elite Mini M2 Air-304 Mini PC on 2026-11-16 and described it as Minisforum's smallest and cheapest mini PC packing Intel's Wildcat Lake silicon ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)). The newsroom's verdict is that the machine is small, quiet and easy to stow away, but that its performance is really only for light workloads ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)). The barebones version starts at $263, while the tested configuration with 16GB DDR5-5600, a 500GB SSD and Windows 11 Pro is listed at $559 direct ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)).

## Details (as Reported)

Tom's Hardware describes the processor as a 5C/5T CPU with one Cougar Cove P-core and four Darkmont LP E-cores, paired with an integrated GPU that has a single Xe3 core ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)). The review calls the machine pleasant to use, reports that it ran quietly, and says it only strained in gaming and productivity benchmarks ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)). Its reasons to buy include compact size, low power use, and enough capability for home office and media consumption ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)). Its reasons to avoid include weak creator-task performance, a plastic top shell, and playability limited to old or lightweight games and emulators ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)).

Minisforum lists noise of 27.9 dB at idle and 32.7 dB at 100% load at 50cm in Balance Mode, and 27 dB at idle and 28.1 dB at 100% load in Silent Mode ([Minisforum](https://store.minisforum.com/products/minisforum-m2-air-mini-pc)). The company gives a TDP of 28W in Balance Mode and 15W in Silent Mode, plus an NPU peak of 15 TOPS (Int8) ([Minisforum](https://store.minisforum.com/products/minisforum-m2-air-mini-pc)). Tom's Hardware lists the processor as 15W in its specification table ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)).

On memory, Minisforum states that the system supports up to 6400 MT/s and ships at 5600 MT/s, with a maximum of 64 GB ([Minisforum](https://store.minisforum.com/products/minisforum-m2-air-mini-pc)). Storage is one M.2 2280 NVMe slot running at PCIe 4.0 x4 ([Minisforum](https://store.minisforum.com/products/minisforum-m2-air-mini-pc)). The box includes a 65W power brick that Tom's Hardware calls surprisingly bulky, and the PC can also be powered over USB-C ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/desktops/mini-pcs/minisforum-elite-mini-m2-air-304-review)).
