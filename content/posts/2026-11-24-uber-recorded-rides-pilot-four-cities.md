Uber began piloting Recorded Rides in the U.S. on November 23, an option that lets riders book trips in which the driver has turned on the company's Record My Ride feature ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/uber-now-lets-you-book-rides-that-record-the-drive-by-default/)). Louis, and Uber plans to expand it to more locations later ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/uber-now-lets-you-book-rides-that-record-the-drive-by-default/)). Annie Duvnjak, Uber's global head of safety, said in a press release that knowing a trip is being recorded can give riders and drivers "greater peace of mind, encourage respectful interactions, and deter inappropriate behavior" ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325472/uber-now-lets-riders-request-drivers-who-are-recording-their-trips/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Recorded Rides builds on Record My Ride, an in-app function that Engadget describes as using the driver phone's selfie camera and microphone to record trips ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325472/uber-now-lets-riders-request-drivers-who-are-recording-their-trips/)). TechCrunch describes the feature as using the front camera of the driver's phone ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/uber-now-lets-you-book-rides-that-record-the-drive-by-default/)).

Uber is testing recorded rides specifically for rides booked late at night or those involving teens ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/uber-now-lets-you-book-rides-that-record-the-drive-by-default/)). Drivers can opt out of recording through the Settings panel, and riders can decline permission through an in-app notification ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/uber-now-lets-you-book-rides-that-record-the-drive-by-default/)).

Duvnjak explained that recordings are encrypted, stored on the user's device, and automatically deleted unless uploaded as part of a safety report or insurance claim, and that Uber can only review recordings ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325472/uber-now-lets-riders-request-drivers-who-are-recording-their-trips/)). She added that Recorded Rides will offer "greater confidence that a recording will be available if something doesn't go as planned" ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325472/uber-now-lets-riders-request-drivers-who-are-recording-their-trips/)).

Uber introduced a live video streaming feature for rides booked for teenagers earlier this year, and in another test enabled audio recording for late-night trips in some cities ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/uber-now-lets-you-book-rides-that-record-the-drive-by-default/)). Engadget reported that the live video tool lets parents or legal guardians monitor a teen's trip, and that Uber then expanded it with the Uber Kids program for riders between 10 and 12 years old ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325472/uber-now-lets-riders-request-drivers-who-are-recording-their-trips/)).

- Engadget reported that Uber is also looking into turning on recording in emergencies, such as when RideCheck detects a possible crash or a trip goes "significantly off course" ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2325472/uber-now-lets-riders-request-drivers-who-are-recording-their-trips/)).
