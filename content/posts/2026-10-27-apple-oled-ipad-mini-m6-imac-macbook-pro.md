Apple unveiled an OLED iPad mini on Monday and an M6 iMac and 14-inch MacBook Pro on Tuesday, ahead of its Pro and Tell event ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). The iPad mini starts at $699 and the iMac at $1,499, with pre-orders opening October 28 and in-store availability from November 6 ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)).

## Details (as Reported)

The eighth-generation iPad mini has an 8.5-inch OLED display with a peak brightness of 1,200 nits and a 2,000,000:1 contrast ratio ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). Base storage stays at 128GB, and a nano-texture glass option is offered with 256GB or 512GB at extra cost ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). The tablet carries an IP67 rating and has no Face ID ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)).

The A20 Pro is the same chipset as in the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo, and Apple says it can run large language models locally ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). Apple's C2 modem in the 5G model is said to deliver up to 50 percent faster data speeds than the previous iPad mini ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread come from the N1 chip ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). Battery life is listed at up to 10 hours over Wi-Fi and nine hours over cellular ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)).

The new iPad mini's price is $150 above the previous model after Apple raised that model's price in June ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)).

The M6 iMac keeps its 24-inch 4.5K Super Retina display and 12MP Center Stage camera ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). Apple says the M6 delivers up to five times faster on-device AI performance than the 2024 M4 iMac ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). A four-port model with a 12-core M6 costs at least $1,699 ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)). The iMac adds the N1 chip and an optional 2.5Gb Ethernet port ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)).

Compared with the M5 model, Apple says the M6 14-inch MacBook Pro offers up to 30 percent faster graphics performance and up to 25 percent faster AI workloads ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2298220/everything-apple-announced-ipad-macbook-pro-and-tell-event/)).
