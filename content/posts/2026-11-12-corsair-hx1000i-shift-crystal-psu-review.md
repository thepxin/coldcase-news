Tom's Hardware published its review of the Corsair HX1000i Shift Crystal 1000W power supply on 2026-11-12, the date it gives for the unit's launch ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)). Its verdict calls the unit a showpiece with excellent efficiency, tight regulation and low ripple, and notes that the fanless window covers half of its rated output at room temperature ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)). Against that, the review lists Taiwanese capacitors, a noisy hot run and a 200 mm long chassis ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)). The company's product page currently lists the unit at $339.99 ([Corsair (official statement)](https://go.corsair.com/c/221109/490888/8513?subId1=tomshardware-us-5383644663121128375&sharedId=tomshardware-us&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.corsair.com%2Fus%2Fen%2Fp%2Fpsu%2FCP-9020315-NA%2Fhx1000i-shift-crystal-fully-modular-high-performance-atx-power-supply-cp-9020315-na)).

## Details (as Reported)

Corsair describes a built-in PinProtect+ that monitors current across each 12V pin on the 12V-2x6 connection and limits GPU power if excessive current persists ([Corsair (official statement)](https://go.corsair.com/c/221109/490888/8513?subId1=tomshardware-us-5383644663121128375&sharedId=tomshardware-us&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.corsair.com%2Fus%2Fen%2Fp%2Fpsu%2FCP-9020315-NA%2Fhx1000i-shift-crystal-fully-modular-high-performance-atx-power-supply-cp-9020315-na)). Alongside it, the company lists an integrated iCUE LINK System Hub that connects up to 24 iCUE LINK devices, and a 140mm Magnetic Dome bearing fan with a zero RPM mode ([Corsair (official statement)](https://go.corsair.com/c/221109/490888/8513?subId1=tomshardware-us-5383644663121128375&sharedId=tomshardware-us&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.corsair.com%2Fus%2Fen%2Fp%2Fpsu%2FCP-9020315-NA%2Fhx1000i-shift-crystal-fully-modular-high-performance-atx-power-supply-cp-9020315-na)). Steady output reaches up to 91% efficiency, and the fan ramps up automatically under heavy loads ([Corsair (official statement)](https://go.corsair.com/c/221109/490888/8513?subId1=tomshardware-us-5383644663121128375&sharedId=tomshardware-us&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.corsair.com%2Fus%2Fen%2Fp%2Fpsu%2FCP-9020315-NA%2Fhx1000i-shift-crystal-fully-modular-high-performance-atx-power-supply-cp-9020315-na)). The page also notes that 30mm of clearance between the power supply and the side panel is required ([Corsair (official statement)](https://go.corsair.com/c/221109/490888/8513?subId1=tomshardware-us-5383644663121128375&sharedId=tomshardware-us&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.corsair.com%2Fus%2Fen%2Fp%2Fpsu%2FCP-9020315-NA%2Fhx1000i-shift-crystal-fully-modular-high-performance-atx-power-supply-cp-9020315-na)).

Tom's Hardware reports that the unit is built by CWT, was first shown at Computex 2026, and is rated to deliver its full output continuously at ambient temperatures up to 50°C ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)). At 150 x 86 x 200 mm, the unit has clear polycarbonate panels on three sides ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)). Among its reasons to buy, the reviewer lists Cybenetics Platinum certification, tight voltage regulation, low ripple, fanless operation up to 500W, per-pin 12V-2x6 protection and the ten-year warranty ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)). The bundle contains the AC power cord, four mounting screws, black cable ties and a safety leaflet, which the reviewer calls spartan for the class ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)). Cables include a native 12V-2x6 cable rated for 600W, eight SATA connectors and eight Molex connectors ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/power-supplies/corsair-hx1000i-shift-crystal-power-supply-review)).
