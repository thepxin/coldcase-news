Elon Musk accused Ukraine's military of violating Starlink's terms of service by using the civilian service for offensive drone strikes, in a comment on Saturday that followed a SpaceX speed restriction on Ukrainian drones ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/musk-accuses-ukraine-of-violating-starlink-terms-with-deadly-drone-strikes/)). Ukrainian officials denied the accusation, and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said over the weekend that the new restrictions had been lifted ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/musk-accuses-ukraine-of-violating-starlink-terms-with-deadly-drone-strikes/)).

## Details (as Reported)

Serhii Beskrestnov wrote that all Starlink terminals over Ukrainian territory, including territories occupied by Russia, were already subject to a movement speed limit of 70 to 90 kilometers per hour, and that Starlink does not function over Russian territory ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/musk-accuses-ukraine-of-violating-starlink-terms-with-deadly-drone-strikes/)).

Another former Ukrainian advisor, Serhii Sternenko, wrote that a terminal working on the move had to be added to a special list and connected to the military Starshield system, so all drone missions needing satellite communication could only use Starshield ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1019879/elon-musk-starlink-ukraine-terms-of-service)). Ukrainian officials have accused Musk of throttling their use of Starshield, the military variant that Ukraine accesses through a deal with the US government ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/musk-accuses-ukraine-of-violating-starlink-terms-with-deadly-drone-strikes/)).

Axios reported that Ukraine uses Starlink for communications, logistics and defense against incoming drones, and Starshield for offensive missions, according to officials ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1019879/elon-musk-starlink-ukraine-terms-of-service)). Musk pointed to a Zelenskyy post that referred to Starlink and mid-range strikes against enemy military targets ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1019879/elon-musk-starlink-ukraine-terms-of-service)).

The Kyiv Independent reports that Ukraine's military can now connect to devices traveling at more than 400kmh (248mph) ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1019879/elon-musk-starlink-ukraine-terms-of-service)).

President Donald Trump responded that he wasn't involved and didn't know who throttled SpaceX's services, and The Wall Street Journal later reported that Trump asked Musk to lift the restrictions ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1019879/elon-musk-starlink-ukraine-terms-of-service)).
